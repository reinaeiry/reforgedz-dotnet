Server Rules

The rules for every ReforgedZ server and the Discord, covering cheating, alts, combat logging, safe zones, group size, bases, raiding and tickets.

By joining or playing on a ReforgedZ server, you agree to these rules. Not knowing a rule is no excuse, and staff decisions are final. Survival is the goal, but fun is the mission: play smart and play fair.

Cheating and exploits

  1. No cheating or exploiting. Hacks, cheats, macros and exploits of any kind get a permanent ban. Everyone competes legitimately.
  2. Report bugs; don’t use them. Report a bug or glitch in a ticket straight away. Exploiting one for your own gain is treated the same as cheating.
  3. One account per player. Alt accounts are banned. Anyone found using, owning or benefiting from one, whether for scouting, storing loot, getting around a punishment or any other advantage, is banned permanently.

Conduct

  1. Zero tolerance for harassment and hate. No racist, homophobic, sexist, hateful, defamatory, threatening or baiting language or behavior, including discriminatory jokes and targeted harassment. It’s an immediate permanent ban, with no warning.
  2. Keep chat clean. A message with prohibited language is blocked, and you’re muted for 1 week: You have been muted for 1 week for using prohibited language.
  3. No metagaming. Don’t use Discord, streams, chats, DMs or stream sniping to find or share where players or bases are. Don’t call out anyone’s location or base in any chat, in game included. You may only give out your own location or your own base’s.
  4. Respect staff. Staff volunteer their free time to run the community you’re playing in. Disrespect toward any staff member costs you the right to talk on our platforms, up to a permanent mute, reviewed case by case by the Administration Team. If you disagree with a staff member or a decision, report it through the proper channels.

Fights

  1. Max group size is 10 players per side, in anything: roaming, PvP, raiding, defending, counter-raiding and holding an area.
    • Alliances are fine for diplomacy and trade, but everyone on one side of a fight or an area counts toward the 10.
    • Separate groups, different voice channels or chains of allies still count as one side.
    • Two groups of 5 raiding together is fine. A group of 6 with 5 allies nearby escorting or scouting is not. If extra players arrive, back off at once.
  2. Leave fresh spawns alone. No spawn camping and no hunting players who have just spawned. A fresh spawn who starts the fight loses that protection.
  3. Don’t combat log. After you die, wait at least 5 seconds and see the death screen before you log out. Logging out at once to despawn your body, or logging out during a fight, is combat logging. See Death and Combat Logging.

Punishments

Cheating, alt accounts, harassment and hate are a permanent ban straight away. Combat logging and metagaming climb:

First offense Second offense After that
Combat logging 24-hour ban 1-week ban Permanent ban
Metagaming 24-hour ban 1-week ban Permanent ban, for repeated or severe cases

Safe zones

  1. No killing, looting, vehicle theft or camping just outside safe zones.
  2. Let people come and go. Don’t block entrances, and don’t trap, block or grief anyone in a safe zone.
  3. Safe zones are not garages. Unattended vehicles in a safe zone are deleted, so don’t log out and leave one there.

Bases

  1. One flag per player or group. Over-limit bases are removed without warning. No claiming or taking over entire towns.
  2. Base funds keep your base alive. Upkeep is charged from them once a day. A base that runs dry gets 24 hours of grace, then it is abandoned (its codelocks reset to 0000 and it is open to loot for 4 hours) and removed.
  3. Inactive bases can be wiped. A base inactive for 7 days or more may be wiped or removed, and inactive players risk losing stored items and gear.
  4. Don’t build in restricted places:
    • Not in the town of Balota, on Chernarus.
    • Not in or blocking high loot areas, roads or routes.
    • Not within 400 m of a point of interest: military zones, safe zones, bandit camps and strongholds, castles, sawmills, bunkers, the Drug Processor or the Black Market.
    • Not where staff have placed a Can’t Build Here Mate sign. Players can’t pick the sign up or destroy it.
  5. Some buildings can be taken over and some can’t. You may take over buildings with Workbenches or Drill Presses, as long as they’re more than 400 m from a point of interest. You may not take over buildings with Furnaces, Sawmills, Market Terminals, Key Lockers, Fallen Soldiers or Major Loot Cabinets.
  6. No floating, glitched, unrealistic or excessively stacked structures. No sky bases, floating platforms or unraidable designs. Bases more than 5 levels above the ground are removed.

Danger

A base that breaks these rules is deleted with everything in it, with no compensation.

Vehicles and helicopters

Helicopters take a massive grind, so targeted griefing isn’t allowed.

  1. Owners: don’t place building pieces through or around any vehicle to glitch it, or to stop other players lockpicking it and driving or flying it away.
  2. Everyone else: don’t go to another player’s base just to blow up their helicopter and leave. Destroying a helicopter must be part of a real, active raid on that base.

Raiding

  1. Raiding is allowed and encouraged. No glitches or exploits during raids. Use only approved raid tools and explosives. See Raiding and Defending.

Tickets, evidence and reimbursement

  1. Use tickets for support. Don’t DM or tag staff.
  2. Bring evidence. Reimbursement requests and cheating reports need clear video or photos, so record at least the last 3 minutes of gameplay. Unclear or missing evidence means the ticket is denied: no evidence, no reimbursement.
  3. Cheating reports must give the server, the in-game name, the approximate time and the evidence. False reports are punished.
  4. Reimbursement is only for bugs, glitches or abnormal server events. Nothing is reimbursed for restarts, rollbacks, bad storage or player error.

On the Discord

  • Respect everyone.
  • Privacy: no sharing personal information, private DMs or photos, and no doxxing.
  • No spam or advertising, and no links to other servers or Discords.
  • No NSFW or gore, including profile pictures and banners.
  • English only in public channels.
  • No political or religious discussion.
