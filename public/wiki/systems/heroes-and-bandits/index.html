Heroes and Bandits

Your reputation comes from what you do to other players. The nine ranks, what moves you between them, and every perk and penalty a rank brings.

On this page
  • The nine ranks
  • What moves it
  • Where you see it
  • Hero perks
  • Bandit perks
  • How you spawn

Everything you do to other players moves one number. Help people and you climb toward Hero. Murder them and you fall toward Bandit. Zombies, bandits and soldiers don’t move it at all.

Your rank is worth more than the title. It changes your tax, how fast you craft, build, farm and fish, how much damage you take, and how well you pick a lock.

You start Neutral, at 0
It moves when You kill, heal or rob other players, and when you finish contracts and missions
It never moves for Killing zombies, bandits or soldiers, or for dying
You see it On the REPUTATION and PERKS tabs in your inventory

The nine ranks

Side Rank You reach it at
Hero Guardian +2,500
Protector +20,000
Savior +50,000
Legend +100,000
Neutral Neutral Between −2,500 and +2,500. Everyone starts here
Bandit Outlaw −2,500
Raider −20,000
Marauder −50,000
Warlord −100,000
  • You only pick a side at 2,500, in either direction. Below that you’re Neutral whatever you’ve been up to.
  • A side sticks. Once you’re a Hero you stay one until you drop under +1,000, and a Bandit stays a Bandit until they climb over −1,000. Your rank won’t flicker while you sit on the line.

What moves it

What you do Which way it moves
Murder a Neutral player 500 toward Bandit
Murder a Hero 200 to 500 toward Bandit, more for a higher rank
Kill a Bandit 200 to 500 toward Hero, more for a higher rank
Betray your own side (a Hero kills a Hero, a Bandit kills a Bandit) 1,000 toward the other side
Kill someone who was attacking another player 250 toward Hero
Kill someone who just attacked you Nothing. Self-defence is free
Bandage another player 50 toward Hero
Give another player morphine 75 toward Hero
Give another player saline 250 toward Hero
Lockpick a vehicle 100 toward Bandit
Finish a contract Hero or bandit reputation. See Contracts
Kill a zombie, a bandit or a soldier Nothing
Die, however it happens Nothing
  • Self-defence is free. If the player you killed damaged you in the last 5 minutes, the kill doesn’t move your reputation either way.
  • The same player is worth less the second time. The first kill or heal of a player counts in full, the second counts half, and after that they’re worth nothing for an hour. It cuts both ways, so you can’t farm a friend for reputation.
  • Stopping an attacker pays. If your target had hurt another player in the last 5 minutes, killing them pays 250 toward Hero, whoever they were.

Where you see it

Open your inventory. REPUTATION shows your rank and your kill record, and PERKS lists everything your rank is giving you right now. Both update as soon as your rank changes.

Heroes also pay less tax at every trader: 15% as a Guardian, 10% as a Protector or Savior and 5% as a Legend, against the 20% a Neutral pays. They pay less for base radius upgrades too, and from Protector up less for fuel and lower garage fees. See Traders and Caps.

How you spawn

Every rank has its own clothes. Neutral spawns in a T-shirt, jeans and sneakers. Hero ranks spawn in combat shirts and trousers with jungle boots, and a Legend in a leather jacket, cowboy hat, gloves and a rucksack. Bandit ranks spawn in bomber jackets, and a Warlord in a camouflaged uniform with a neck gaiter and gloves. Every Hero and Bandit kit adds a flashlight. See Your First Hour.
