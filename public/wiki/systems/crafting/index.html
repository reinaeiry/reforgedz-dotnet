Crafting

Craft small things anywhere from your inventory, and everything else at a workbench. Where to craft, what it costs you, and where the item ends up.

On this page

  • Wooden planks

There are two ways to craft. Small things like rope, rags, planks and opened cans you make from your own inventory, anywhere. Everything else, from ammunition to base kits and vehicles, is made at a workbench. The two have separate recipe lists, so a recipe on one never shows up on the other.

Inventory crafting Workbench
Where Anywhere At a Crafting Workbench
Materials come from Your inventory The workbench first, then your inventory
The item goes Into your inventory, or at your feet if it’s full Into the workbench’s storage
Can you walk away? No, you stay crouched until it’s done Yes, the timer keeps running
Hero and Bandit perks No effect Change the craft time

Every recipe, with ingredients and times, is on the Recipes page.

Crafting from your inventory

Open your inventory. There are three ways to craft from it.

  1. The Crafts tab. It lists 16 recipes in alphabetical order. A recipe you can make shows in white; one you can’t shows in grey. Each line shows what it needs, how many of each you’re carrying, and any tool.
  2. Drag one item onto another. Drag a tin onto a Tin Opener to open it, or a pumpkin onto a knife to slice it. Either item can be the one you drag. There are 33 of these, and opening a can only works this way, not from the Crafts tab.
  3. Unpack. Select a Box of 20 Nails and press F, or A on a controller, to get 20 Nails in 2 seconds.

While the craft runs, a countdown shows on screen and your character crouches.

  • Cancel any time with Esc, or B on a controller. Nothing is used up until the timer finishes, so a cancelled craft costs you nothing.
  • Keep the ingredients on you. The game checks again when the timer ends. If you dropped something, the craft fails.
  • Other players can hear you. The crafting sound plays where you’re standing, for everyone nearby.
  • A full inventory drops the item at your feet. Pick it up: items left on the ground disappear after 10 minutes.

Tools

A tool must be in your inventory, and it’s never used up.

Tool Used for
Knife (any bayonet works) Cutting clothing into rags, seeds, pumpkins, stabbing cans open
Tin Opener Opening cans with nothing spilled
Hacksaw Sawing a Wooden Log into planks
Mallet Nailing a baseball bat

Cutting clothing into rags needs a jacket or trousers you aren’t wearing, with nothing inside. It gives 2 Cloth in 3 seconds.

Open cans with a Tin Opener, not a knife. A Tin Opener takes 2 seconds and gives a full can. Stabbing it open with a knife takes 3 seconds and spills half.

Wooden planks

Planks are the main material for wooden base kits, furniture and crates, and every plank starts as a log from a tree.

  1. Chop down a tree with an axe. It falls after about 4 hits with a Fire Axe, or about 6 with a Tomahawk. Other weapons and vehicles never fell a tree.
  2. Pick up the logs. A fallen tree drops 1 to 3 Wooden Logs, depending on its size, plus 4 Sticks. Logs left on the ground disappear after 10 minutes.
  3. Turn the logs into planks, one of two ways:
Where Needs Makes Time
Crafts tab, anywhere 1 Wooden Log and a Hacksaw 2 Wooden plank 30 s
Sawmill 1 Wooden Log 6 Wooden plank, dropped beside the saw 10 s
  • A sawmill gives three times as many planks. No sawmill stands on either map, so build your own at your base from Sawmill plans. Traders and the Black Market sell the plans.
  • The Black Market sells Fire Axes and Tomahawks.
  • No trader buys or sells planks or logs. You make every one yourself.
  • Salvage gives planks back. Salvaging a wooden kit you crafted returns half its planks. See Salvage.

Crafting at a workbench

Getting a workbench

  • Use one in the world. Public workbenches are marked on your map with the Workbench icon. See Map Icons.
  • Place your own. Traders sell a Workbench. Hold it in your hands to place it. Pack up Workbench (hold for 5 seconds) turns it back into the item, and moving a placed one needs your building tool.

Using the menu

Walk up and use Open Crafting Menu.

  1. Pick a category, then a recipe. Recipes that make more than one item show the amount in the name, like Nails x10.
  2. Check the required items. Anything you don’t have enough of turns red.
  3. Set how many with Z and C, or LT and RT on a controller. You can queue up to 999 in one craft, and Time to craft shows the total.
  4. Hold Enter to craft, or hold A on a controller. A quick tap doesn’t start it.
  • Ingredients come from the workbench first, then from you. You can split the materials between the bench and your backpack.
  • One craft at a time per workbench. A second one won’t start until the first finishes.
  • You don’t have to wait. The timer runs in real time whether you stay or not.
  • Finished items go into the workbench, not your hands. Use the Transfer tab to take them out.
  • Some materials are spent a use at a time. A Sewing Kit has 4 uses, Adhesive 3 and each Spraypaint 5. A recipe asking for 1 use takes one use, not the whole item.

Warning

Anything left inside a workbench is lost at the next server restart. The crafting menu warns you about it too. Take your items out before you log off. See Server Restarts.

Heroes craft faster

Your humanity changes how long workbench crafts take. Heroes craft up to 30% faster as they rank up (0%, 10%, 20%, then 30%). Bandits are up to 30% slower (5%, 10%, 20%, then 30%). The menu always shows the normal time. Inventory crafts are not affected. See Heroes and Bandits.

Other stations

These use the same crafting menu as a workbench:

  • Concrete Mixer (Use Concrete Mixer, marked Mixer on your map). Makes Mortar Mix from 4 Sandbags and 2 Water, and Bricks x10 from 20 Stone and 2 Water. Each takes 2 minutes.

For these, you hold the prompt:

  • Sawmill (Process wooden log). 1 Wooden Log becomes 6 planks in 10 seconds, three times what a hacksaw gives. The planks drop next to the saw. See Wooden planks.
  • Furnace. Smelt Iron Ore turns 5 Iron Nuggets into 1 Iron Bar in 10 seconds. Smelt Gold Ore turns 10 Gold Nuggets into 1 Gold Ingot in 25 seconds. The nuggets come from Mining.
  • Washing Machine. Dyes a Crafted Armband with one Fabric Dye in 2 seconds: black, blue, green, orange, pink, red or yellow. Make the plain armband from 1 Cloth in the Crafts tab.
