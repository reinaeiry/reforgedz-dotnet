Contracts

The Broker in the safe zone offers daily and weekly contracts. Take one of each, do the work, walk back and get paid.

Contracts are paid jobs from The Broker, who stands in the safe zone. Each day you’re offered three dailies, and each week three weeklies. You take one of each, do the work anywhere on the map, then walk back to him to get paid.

Who The Broker, in the safe zone
Offered to you 1 neutral, 1 hero and 1 bandit daily, and the same three weeklies
You can take 1 daily and 1 weekly
Dailies reset 00:00 UTC every day
Weeklies reset Monday 00:00 UTC
Range Within 10 m of The Broker to accept or hand in
Pays Caps, standing, reputation, and often items

Taking a contract

  1. Walk up to The Broker and use Contracts — The Broker.
  2. Pick one. The menu lists DAILY CONTRACTS and WEEKLY CONTRACTS, each with a countdown to its reset (3h 12m, 2d 4h). Select a contract to see its objectives and rewards.
  3. Accept it. You’ll see CONTRACT ACCEPTED.

The moment you take one, the others in that section grey out: LOCKED - you have already taken a daily contract today. Come back after the next reset.

Warning

You can’t change your mind. Daily and weekly contracts cannot be abandoned. Read the objectives before you accept.

What you’re offered

  • Your offers are your own. Every player gets their own three dailies and three weeklies.
  • Hero and bandit contracts are open to everyone. Your reputation doesn’t limit what you can take. It’s the reward that moves you: a hero contract pays hero reputation, a bandit contract pays bandit reputation.
  • Nothing you can’t do. Contracts for places on another map never come up, and contracts that need more standing aren’t offered until you have it.

Doing the work

Track progress on the Quests tab of your inventory, under ACTIVE CONTRACTS. Each finished objective shows OBJECTIVE COMPLETE.

Kill objectives

A kill objective names a target: infected, bandits, soldiers, any player, or players on the Hero side. Many add a condition, and a kill that misses it doesn’t count.

Condition What counts
At night Kills between 20:00 and 06:00, in-game time
With a weapon type The weapon in your hands is a rifle, sniper rifle, machine gun or handgun
From a distance You were at least the stated distance from the target
At a place Your target dies inside the named town or site

Named places are circles around the town, not its full sprawl. The edge of town can be outside the circle.

Other objectives

  • Visit a place: Visit Vybor. It completes when you arrive.
  • Deliver: carry the items to the named place. They’re taken from you when you arrive. Only full, unused items count.
  • Hand over: bring the items to The Broker and press Hand Over. He takes what you’re carrying, up to what’s still needed, and keeps count, so you can bring them over several trips. Only full, unused items count: an opened drink or a part-eaten can is refused with Those are not full - I need full ones.
  • Activity counts: these tick up as you play.
Activity Counts
Fishing Fish caught. Some contracts want one species.
Farming Crops harvested
Crafting and salvage Items crafted, items salvaged
Hunting Animals skinned
Trading Items sold to a trader, or caps earned from traders
Fuel Litres delivered to fuel pumps out in the world
Driving Whole kilometres driven, as the driver
Missions Missions completed while you’re there, and supply drops looted
Locks Vehicle locks picked
Bunkers Bunker doors opened with a keycard

Handing in

Walk back to The Broker, open the menu, select the finished contract and hand it in. You’ll see CONTRACT COMPLETE. If something’s missing: Objectives not complete.

Missed the reset? If every objective is finished, the contract waits for you and you can still hand it in after the reset. A hand-over counts as finished once The Broker has taken every item. Unfinished progress is lost at the reset, including items you already handed over.

Standing

Every contract but one pays standing with The Broker, a score that’s separate from your hero or bandit reputation. More standing opens harder, better-paying contracts.

  • Dailies pay +1 or +2 standing. Weeklies pay +5.
  • New tiers open at 6, 15, 18 and 40 standing.
  • The menu shows your standing and how far it is to the next tier, or Top tier reached.

Rewards

Dailies Weeklies
Caps Yes Yes, much more
Standing +1 or +2 +5
Hero reputation (hero contracts) +180 to +480 +900 to +1,700
Bandit reputation (bandit contracts) 100 to 500 900 to 2,200
Items One pays a second Mystery weapon (Tier 1-2) Many pay a Barrel, Large Military Case, Large Military Case Codelocked, Level 03 Keycard or Level 04 Keycard
Bonus on every contract A random weapon from the lower tiers A random weapon from the higher tiers

The menu lists the daily bonus as Mystery weapon (Tier 1-2) and the weekly bonus as Mystery weapon (Tier 3-4). The one daily that pays a second item lists it as Mystery weapon (Tier 1-2) too. When you hand in, a REWARDS message names exactly what you got.

Keycards and bunkers are explained on Keys and Locked Containers.

How you lose a payout

  • Taking the wrong contract. You can’t abandon it, and it locks the others until the reset.
  • Killing outside the circle. At-a-place kills only count inside it.
  • The wrong weapon in your hands. Weapon-type kills check what you’re holding.
  • Bringing opened items. A hand-over only takes full, unused items.
  • Handing over part of the order, then missing the reset. Items he has taken are gone, and the unfinished contract is lost.
