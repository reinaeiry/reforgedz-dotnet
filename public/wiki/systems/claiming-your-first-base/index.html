Claiming Your First Base

Plant a flag, keep caps in its wallet, and build inside its circle. What a flag gives you, what it costs, and how bases are lost.

On this page

  • Planting the flag
  • Building inside your circle

  • Members
  • Tent bases

A base starts with a flag. Plant it, keep caps in its wallet, and everything within 25 m of it is yours to build on. Let the wallet run dry and the base is thrown open to everyone, then deleted.

You need A Flag Kit and a Building Tool
Territory 25 m around the flag, upgradable to 35 m or 50 m
Size limit 200 base parts
Upkeep 800 caps a day at 25 m, plus your parts. The 25 cheapest parts are free.
Members Up to 10 players, owner included

What you need

  • Flag Kit. Craft it from 1 Wooden plank, 1 Nuts & Bolts and 1 Ripstop Fabric (30 s). Traders sell it too.
  • Building Tool. Nothing gets built without one in your hands. It turns up as loot, and the Black Market sells it.
  • Caps. The first day’s upkeep comes out of the flag’s wallet, so bring some.

Planting the flag

  1. Hold the Flag Kit and use it, R by default, or the right trigger on a controller. A preview of the flag follows your aim.
  2. Place it on solid ground. A red preview means you can’t put it there.
  3. Build it. Take the Building Tool in your hands and hit the flag until it’s finished.

You can own one flag. A second gives You already own a flag. If you’re already a member of someone else’s base, you can’t plant one of your own: You already belong to a base.

Where you can’t plant

Message Why
Cannot place a flag within 60m of another flag Flags must be at least 60 m apart
Another base controls this area. You cannot place a flag here. You’re inside someone else’s territory
Building is blocked in this area Some places refuse all building

The server rules add more: no bases in Balota on Chernarus, and none within 400 m of military zones, safe zones, bandit camps and other points of interest. A base that breaks the rules is deleted by staff. See Server Rules.

Building inside your circle

Everything you build is placed from a kit, in two steps: put the kit down as a see-through plan, then hit the plan with the Building Tool until it’s finished.

  • Open build mode with the Building Tool in your hands: B, or the right trigger on a controller. Every key is on Controls.
  • Stay inside the circle. Outside it you’ll see Outside your base area (within 25 m of your flag).
  • See the circle. The flag has a Show Flag Radius action that draws your territory ring on the ground.
  • Take a piece back with Remove Base Part, and you get its kit back. Storage has to be empty first. A built-in lock keeps its tier in the kit: Tier 2 lock stored in the kit.
  • 200 parts, then stop. The base menu shows Base Entities: 120 / 200. At the limit: Max base entities reached.

Per-base limits

Kind Most per base
Generator 1
Greenhouse, Large Vegetable Bed and Small Vegetable Bed, combined 4

Try to place one more and you’ll see Base limit reached, with the limit.

Upkeep: the flag’s wallet

Your base pays for itself out of a wallet on the flag. Open the flag’s base menu, go to FUNDS and deposit caps from your inventory. Any member can deposit.

  • The bill. 800 caps a day for a 25 m territory, plus a daily cost for every part. The 25 cheapest parts cost nothing. The UPKEEP tab lists your bill by category.
  • When it’s charged. Once every 24 hours, starting the day you plant the flag. There’s no free period.
  • Wallet limit. 150,000 caps. Deposit more and you’ll see Base funds full!
  • You can’t outbuild your wallet. Past 25 parts, a new piece only goes down if the wallet can cover a day’s bill with it: Cannot build - fund your flag wallet first!

Bigger territory

Any member can buy a bigger radius on the BASE RADIUS tab. It raises the daily bill in place of the 800.

Radius One-off cost Daily territory cost
25 m Free 800 caps
35 m 40,000 caps 2,500 caps
50 m 100,000 caps 5,000 caps

Heroes pay less for radius upgrades.

How you lose a base

The base menu warns you before anything happens.

  1. LOW FUNDS. Under two days of caps left, the menu reads LOW FUNDS - 1d 6h remaining! Deposit caps soon, and under 12 hours in hours and minutes: LOW FUNDS - 9h 40m remaining! Deposit caps soon. If the wallet holds less than one day’s bill, it names the bill and when it’s due instead: LOW FUNDS - next bill 800 caps in 5h 12m! Deposit caps.
  2. BASE AT RISK. When a daily bill comes due and the wallet can’t pay all of it, members online see Your base wallet is empty! Deposit caps or your base will expire. A bill the wallet pays in full never starts this, even if it leaves the wallet at 0. You have 24 hours to deposit. The menu counts down: BASE AT RISK - Deposit funds! Expires in 23h 10m.
  3. ABANDONED. Once those 24 hours are up, the base is abandoned. Every codelock opens, the owner and every member are removed, the whole server gets an ABANDONED BASE banner, and an Abandoned Base marker goes on everyone’s map. Anyone can walk in and take what they like.
  4. Deleted. At least 4 hours after it is abandoned, the flag and everything built around it is gone.

A deposit that covers a day’s bill, made at any point before step 3, saves the base.

Danger

An abandoned base is announced to every player on the server and marked on their maps. Keep a few days of caps in the wallet.

Members

Open the flag’s base menu (the action reads Base Managment) to manage who can use your base. Only members can open it.

Owner Manager Member
Build, open locked doors, deposit caps Yes Yes Yes
Add a nearby player Yes Yes No
Remove, promote or demote a member Yes No No
Hand the base to someone else Yes No No
  • Up to 10 players, owner included. After that: Member limit reached.
  • One base per player. You can’t add someone who owns a base or belongs to another one.
  • Leaving is always allowed. A member can take themselves off the list.
  • Change the flag on the CHANGE FLAG tab. There are 16 designs at 100 caps each, and designs you own are marked (OWNED).
  • Base members don’t need codes. Inside your own base, your codelocks let you straight through. See Codelocks.

Tent bases

A Tent Bag sets up a smaller base without a flag: a 20 m circle and room for 15 parts. It follows the same upkeep rules, but its radius can’t be upgraded and its flag design can’t be changed.
