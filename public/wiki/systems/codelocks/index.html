Codelocks

Lock doors, hatches, gates and storage with a 4-digit code. Lock tiers, codes, lockouts, and why base members never need the code.

A codelock keeps a door or a box shut to anyone outside your base. Base members walk straight through. Everyone else needs the 4-digit code, or explosives.

Code 4 digits
Who can open it Base members, always. Anyone else with the code.
Tiers 1 to 3. Higher tiers take more explosives to break.
Wrong codes Too many and the lock refuses you for a while

Two kinds of lock

  • The Codelock. An item you fit to a door or to a lockable box after it’s built. Craft it from 1 Iron Bar, 1 Electrical Parts, 1 Nuts & Bolts and 1 Plastic Resin Pellets (30 s). It also turns up as loot.
  • Codelocked parts. Doors, floor hatches, garage doors, the palisade gate and some storage come in a (Codelocked) version with the lock built in. Craft one from the plain kit plus a Codelock (20 s), for example Wooden Door Kit Centered (Codelocked).
Fitted Codelock Built-in lock
Wrong codes before lockout 3 5
Lockout 15 minutes 5 minutes
Explosive charges to break: Tier 1 / 2 / 3 4 / 6 / 8 2 / 4 / 6
Repair uses Electrical Toolbox Electrical Toolbox (the action says Toolkit)
If you take it down You get the Codelock back, at its tier Its tier stays in the kit

A built-in lock is cheaper to break into. A fitted Codelock takes twice as many charges at Tier 1.

Fitting a Codelock

  1. Stand inside your own base. Only the owner and members can fit one. Anywhere else: You must be inside your base (owner or member) to place codelocks.
  2. Hold the Codelock and use it, R by default, or the right trigger on a controller. Aim at a door or a lockable box. The placing keys are on Controls.
  3. Set a code straight away. The game prompts Set a 4-digit code for your codelock. A lock with no code doesn’t lock anything.

Lockable boxes include the barrel, the large military crate, and the weapon lockers and racks.

Codes

The keypad takes the number row or the numpad. Enter confirms and Backspace clears. On a controller, move around the keypad and press A.

  • Setting a code. Set Code (Set Lock Code on a built-in lock), type it (Enter new code), then type it again (Confirm code (enter again)).
  • Changing it. Change Code asks for the old code first (Enter previous code), then the new one twice.
  • Locking and unlocking. Members get Lock Door and Unlock Door on a fitted Codelock, or Lock Cabinet and Unlock Cabinet on a built-in lock. Actions show the lock’s state, for example Enter Code [LOCKED].

Members don’t need the code

If you’re a member of the base a lock stands in, the lock treats you as allowed. You open locked doors without typing anything, and on a fitted Codelock Enter Code doesn’t even appear for you. Adding someone to the base is the proper way to let them in. See Claiming Your First Base.

Giving the code to someone else

A player who isn’t a member can use Enter Code. The right code unlocks the lock for everyone, and it stays unlocked. Only a member can lock it again, so a friend you let in can’t lock the door behind them.

Warning

After someone outside the base uses your code, the door stays open until a member locks it. Change the code if it has spread.

Getting locked out

Enter too many wrong codes and the lock refuses you: You are temporarily locked out. Try again later., then Locked out - 12 min remaining. A built-in lock says Too many failed attempts. Locked out. The lockout applies to you, not to the lock. Your members can still open it.

Lock tiers

Every lock starts at Tier 1. Upgrade it with caps: look at the lock and choose Upgrade Lock (Tier 1 to 2) - Cost: 2000 Caps.

Tier Upgrade cost What it changes
1 Where every lock starts
2 2,000 caps Takes more charges to break. A fitted Codelock adds 40 caps a day to upkeep.
3 5,000 more caps Takes the most charges. A fitted Codelock adds 100 caps a day to upkeep.
  • You can tell the tier by looking. Each tier has its own casing, and a broken lock looks wrecked.
  • Anyone can check a lock’s health. Look at it and the readout shows its health and how many charges it still needs: HP: 400/400 (C4 to destroy: 4).

Damage and repair

  • A fitted Codelock that’s destroyed is gone. The door it was on opens to everyone. Fit a new one.
  • A built-in lock breaks but stays. A broken lock no longer keeps anyone out. It also loses its code and drops back to Tier 1. Carry an Electrical Toolbox and choose Repair Lock (consumes Toolkit), then set a new code. A damaged but unbroken one shows Repair Damage (consumes Toolkit).
  • A fitted Codelock that’s been hit can be patched up by a member carrying an Electrical Toolbox: Repair Codelock (consumes Electrical Toolbox).
  • Pack up a part with a broken lock? Not until it’s repaired: Repair the lock before packing up.

How locks get broken is on Raiding and Defending.

Taking a lock down

  • Remove Codelock takes a fitted Codelock off. Members can do it, and you get the Codelock back at its tier. A damaged one has to be repaired first.
  • Packing up a Codelocked part with Remove Base Part returns its kit, and an upgraded lock’s tier is kept: Tier 3 lock stored in the kit.

When a base is abandoned

If a base runs out of upkeep and is abandoned, every lock in it opens, codes and all. The lock actions disappear, and anyone can walk in until the base is deleted.

Lockpicks are for vehicles

No lockpick opens a codelock. Picks open locked vehicles. There are two: the Lockpick and the Advanced Lockpick. Higher-tier vehicle locks need the Advanced one: You need an Advanced Lockpick to unlock this vehicle. Both turn up as loot.

Picking is a short minigame against the clock. The screen tells you the lock’s tier (TIER 2 LOCK) and how many picks and attempts you have left. Turn the pick to find the angle, then lean on it with tension.

  • Wrong angle, heavy hand: the lock jams (JAMMED - EASE OFF OR FORCE IT), the pick takes damage, and it can snap (PICK BROKE!).
  • Too slow: OUT OF TIME, and you try again.
  • Right angle: UNLOCKED!

A light touch while you search costs you nothing. Forcing it at the wrong angle is what breaks picks. The keys are on Controls.
