Traders and Caps

Caps are money you carry. Traders buy and sell for them, and stock, tax and your reputation move every price.

Caps are the money of ReforgedZ, and they are real items in your pack. Walk up to a trader, take Trade with and the trader’s name, and buy or sell for caps.

Money Caps, carried as notes in your inventory
Where to trade The Safezone and the Black Market, both on your map
Trade range Stay within 8 m of the trader
Most per trade 50 of one item
Prices move with How much the trader has in stock, your tax rate, your reputation

Caps

Caps are items, not a number on your screen. They sit in your inventory like anything else, and if you die, they stay on your body. The inventory screen adds up everything you carry.

Caps come in 12 notes: 1, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 500, 1,000, 5,000, 10,000, 25,000 and 50,000.

  • You get change. Pay with a big note and the trader hands back the difference.
  • A full pack drops money at your feet. If your change, a payout or part of a purchase doesn’t fit, it lands on the ground next to you. Pick it up before you walk away.
  • The Money Exchange trades notes at face value, with no tax. Buy a 50,000 note with small notes, or buy small notes with a big one. It never costs you anything.

Buying

  1. Pick the right trader. Each one deals only in its own categories. The Food trader won’t sell you a rifle.
  2. Switch to Buy with T, or X on a controller.
  3. Choose the item and the amount. - and = change the quantity, or LT and RT on a controller. F searches, or the View button on a controller.
  4. Press B to buy, then confirm, or Y on a controller. The confirm box shows the total, tax included.

You can buy a weapon with its attachments already fitted, or a vehicle with its parts, in one purchase. See Your First Vehicle.

Tax goes on top of the price. The total you pay is the price plus your tax.

Selling

Selling works like a checkout. Nothing is sold until you confirm.

  1. Switch to Sell with T, or X on a controller. The trader lists what you’re carrying that it buys.
  2. Add lines to the sale. Each line shows how many are in the sale and how many you have. Add as many different items as you like.
  3. Confirm. One confirmation sells the whole list for one combined payout: Sell 12 item(s)?
  4. Changed your mind? C clears the list, or the Menu button on a controller.

Tax comes off the payout. What the trader offers is already after tax.

What sells, and how

  • Only full magazines sell. A partly used magazine is refused, and a magazine loaded in a gun doesn’t count.
  • A weapon sells with its attachments. They’re paid for along with it, and the message says how many went.
  • Fish sell at the Food traders, and are paid by weight. A heavy fish is worth more than a light one of the same kind. Sell fish whole. See Fishing.
  • Only the Black Market buys vehicles, and only some. It takes military vehicles, trucks, vans and motorbikes. No trader buys back a car, a boat or a helicopter. Park the vehicle on the trader’s parking spots, carry its key and make sure nobody is inside. A vehicle with parts missing is refused, and the message names what’s missing.
  • If a line can’t be sold, the rest still go through and the message tells you how many lines were skipped.

Why prices move

Every trader has stock, and stock sets the price. When a trader has plenty of something, it charges less for it and pays you less for yours. When a trader is running low, buying costs more and selling pays more.

  • Prices move in steps, not by the cap. A big sale can drop the price a whole step.
  • Big sales stick. Stock never refills or empties on its own. A trader you filled up keeps paying less until players buy its stock back down.
  • A full trader still buys. It just pays the lowest price.
  • If the price changes while you look, the trade stops with Price changed - list refreshed. Check the new price and try again.

Tax and your reputation

Your reputation rank sets your tax rate. It goes on top of everything you buy and comes off everything you sell, at every trader on both maps. Your inventory screen shows your current rate.

Rank Tax
Legend 5%
Savior, Protector 10%
Guardian 15%
Neutral, Outlaw 20%
Raider, Marauder 25%
Warlord 30%

Heroes pay less the higher they climb. An Outlaw pays the same as a Neutral, and every bandit rank above it pays more. Tax is capped, so no reputation makes trading pointless.

Reputation changes more than tax. Every rank and perk is on Heroes and Bandits:

  • Bandits pay less for what they buy from the second bandit rank up, and more so at higher ranks.
  • Bandits get paid more for what they sell, from the second bandit rank up.
  • Heroes get paid less for what they sell. The inventory screen lists this as the cost of a clean name.
  • Heroes pay less for fuel at paid pumps, from the second hero rank up.

Money Exchange notes are never taxed and never discounted.

On Chernarus and Faircroft Islands, both hubs have weapon, clothing, medical, food, general, vehicle and money traders. The Food traders buy fish. On Chernarus the Boat Trader stands on its own, outside both hubs. What each hub has, map by map, is on The Safezone and The Black Market pages.

When a trade fails

Message What it means
This trader does not sell that / does not buy that Wrong trader, or wrong hub for that item
Out of stock / Only 3 in stock The trader has run out, and stays out until players sell it more
Not enough caps (4200 needed) The number includes tax
Price changed - list refreshed Stock moved while you were looking
Spawn point is blocked - move vehicles away Something is parked on the delivery pad
Only the black market buys vehicles Take it to the Black Market
You need this vehicle’s key to sell it Carry the key assigned to it

If a purchase fails after you’ve paid, you get your caps back and a message saying so.
