Systems

What you choose to do: fish, farm, craft, build, trade and drive.

  • FishingWhere your float lands decides what can bite. Bait and the time of day decide how soon.
  • FarmingBeds and greenhouses, what to plant, when to water, how long crops take, and why you sell the harvest in sacks.
  • Fires and CookingBuild a Fireplace, light it, cook on it, sit by it to recover, and keep it from vanishing in the wild.
  • CraftingCraft small things anywhere from your inventory, and everything else at a workbench. Where to craft, what it costs you, and where the item ends up.
  • MiningBreak ore with a Pickaxe at a Mining area and smelt the nuggets at a Furnace. Where to mine on each map, and what each rock gives.
  • SalvageBreak junk down into crafting materials at a Salvage Station, get half back on base kits, and know what's worth the bench time.
  • Claiming Your First BasePlant a flag, keep caps in its wallet, and build inside its circle. What a flag gives you, what it costs, and how bases are lost.
  • StashesBury a Dry Sack to hide gear away from your base. How to bury and dig one up, how long it lasts, and who else can find it.
  • CodelocksLock doors, hatches, gates and storage with a 4-digit code. Lock tiers, codes, lockouts, and why base members never need the code.
  • Raiding and DefendingWalls can't be broken, so every raid goes through a lock. What breaks locks, what warns you when yours are hit, and how to make a raid cost more.
  • DrugsGrow cannabis from seed, process it at a set of drug scales into bags and bricks, and see what each step costs in buds and time.
  • Traders and CapsCaps are money you carry. Traders buy and sell for them, and stock, tax and your reputation move every price.
  • Heroes and BanditsYour reputation comes from what you do to other players. The nine ranks, what moves you between them, and every perk and penalty a rank brings.
  • Your First VehicleBuy or find a vehicle, fit its parts, put your key on it, keep it fuelled and park it in a vehicle terminal.
  • ContractsThe Broker in the safe zone offers daily and weekly contracts. Take one of each, do the work, walk back and get paid.
