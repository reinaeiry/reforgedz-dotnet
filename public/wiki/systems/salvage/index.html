Salvage

Break junk down into crafting materials at a Salvage Station, get half back on base kits, and know what's worth the bench time.

On this page

  • How to salvage
  • What salvaging returns

  • What can’t be salvaged
  • Bandits salvage faster
  • What’s worth the bench time

A Salvage Station turns things you don’t need into crafting materials. A spare rifle becomes Metal Scrap, night vision becomes Electrical Parts, and a base kit you crafted gives back half of what it cost.

Where Fixed stations marked on your map, or build your own at base
Control Salvage Menu at the station, then Hold to Salvage
Base kits Half the materials back, rounded down, at least 1 of each
Speed Most items take 5 to 40 seconds each

Finding a station

  • Fixed stations are marked on your map with the Salvage Station icon: 4 on Chernarus and 2 on Faircroft Islands, one beside each Sawmill. They need no power.
  • Your own station. Buy a Salvage Station plan from a trader or the black market, hold it, and place it inside your base. Packup Salvage Station turns it back into the plan. It doesn’t show on the map.

How to salvage

  1. Open the station with Salvage Menu.
  2. Pick your mode. Transfer moves items between you and the station. Salvage breaks them down.
  3. Select items. The list includes salvageable items in the station and everything you’re carrying, so you don’t have to move anything first. Select as many as you like; the menu shows the combined materials and total time.
  4. Hold to Salvage. The items are used up straight away and the station reads Salvaging… until the job ends.
  5. Collect your materials from the station. They go into the station’s own storage, not your pockets. Use Open Salvage Inventory or the Transfer mode.

One job at a time. While a station is salvaging, nobody else can start a job on it.

Warning

Empty it first. Salvaging a weapon destroys the magazine, sights and attachments mounted on it, and salvaging a vest or backpack destroys everything inside. You only get the materials for the item itself.

Danger

Collect your materials before a restart. A station’s storage is empty after every server restart, and anything left in it is gone.

Each item gives a fixed amount, and weight or condition doesn’t change it.

Getting half back on base kits

Salvage a kit you crafted and you get half its materials back, rounded down, with at least 1 of every material it used. It takes as long as the kit took to craft (at least 5 seconds).

This covers everything from the Base Building, Base Defence, Base Furniture, Storage and Weapons crafting categories. A Wooden Wall Kit that cost 4 Wooden Planks and 6 Nails returns 2 and 3. An item with its own row on this page, such as a Codelock Tier 1, an axe or a pickaxe, gives that row’s amount instead.

Upgraded walls, doors, floors and foundations come back as kits when you remove them, and those salvage back half their upgrade cost too:

Kit Get Time
Metal wall or door kits 2 Corrugated Metal Sheet 15 s
Brick wall or door kits 2 Brick, 1 Mortar Mix 20 s
Concrete wall, door, floor or foundation kits 2 Brick, 2 Stone, 1 Mortar Mix 25 s
Stone Foundation Kit, Stone Floor Kit 5 Stone, 1 Mortar Mix 20 s
Tile Floor Kit 1 Brick, 1 Mortar Mix 20 s

Triangle kits give less: 1 Brick, 1 Stone and 1 Mortar Mix for concrete, and 2 Stone and 1 Mortar Mix for stone.

Nothing else you craft refunds. Clothing, medical items, farming items and materials can’t be salvaged unless they appear in the tables above.

What can’t be salvaged

  • Caps.
  • Crafting materials themselves: you can’t turn scrap into more scrap.
  • Food and most medical items. If an item doesn’t appear in the station’s Salvage list, it can’t be salvaged.

Bandits salvage faster

Your Humanity standing can cut the time a job takes. It never changes how much you get. See Heroes and Bandits.

Standing Salvage time
Outlaw Normal
Raider 15% shorter
Marauder 25% shorter
Warlord 40% shorter

Hero standing doesn’t slow salvaging down.

What’s worth the bench time

  • Metal Scrap: a Jerry Can is the best haul, 10 in 30 seconds. Empty cans are the quickest, 1 every 5 seconds.
  • Electrical Parts: Codelocks and night vision.
  • Nuts & Bolts: vehicle engines and toolboxes.
  • Fabric: backpacks for Ripstop, vests and vehicle covers for Woodland Camo, uniforms for Cotton.
  • Gunpowder and explosive residue: spare magazines and grenades you’ll never use.
  • Kits you built by mistake: half back beats nothing.
