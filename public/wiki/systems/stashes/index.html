Stashes

Bury a Dry Sack to hide gear away from your base. How to bury and dig one up, how long it lasts, and who else can find it.

On this page
  • Burying one
  • Digging it up
  • How long it lasts

A stash is a Dry Sack you bury in the ground. It keeps gear away from your base, where a raid can’t take it, and it doesn’t need a flag, a codelock or upkeep.

You need A Dry Sack and your Building Tool
Burying takes 15 seconds
How many 2 at a time, each player
It lasts 5 days
Who can dig it up Anyone holding a Building Tool

Burying one

  1. Fill the Dry Sack and drop it where you want it. Sacks come in green, orange, red and yellow.
  2. Hold your Building Tool and use Stash. It takes 15 seconds.
  3. The sack becomes a dirt pile. That pile is what other players see, so pick ground where it doesn’t stand out.
  • Two at a time. Bury a third and the game refuses until you dig one up.
  • Only you see it on your map. Your buried stashes show as markers for you alone.

Digging it up

Hold a Building Tool and use Dig Up. Everything you buried comes back in the sack.

Anyone with a Building Tool can dig up any stash they find. A stash is hidden, not locked, so the hiding place is the whole defence. Away from paths, away from your base, and not somewhere you’ve been seen digging.

How long it lasts

5 days from the moment you bury it. After that the server deletes the stash, with everything inside, the next time it sweeps for expired ones. Dig it up and bury it again to start the 5 days over.

What’s inside keeps its condition. Food and drink keep their uses, cooked and rotten stay as they were, batteries keep their charge, cans keep their fuel, a fish keeps its weight, a lockpick keeps its wear, and a weapon comes back with its attachments and magazines in the right slots.
