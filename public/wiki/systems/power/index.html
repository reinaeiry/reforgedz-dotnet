Power and Generators

A generator in your base runs fridges, cookers, lights, a sawmill and the battery charger. How far it reaches, how much fuel it burns and what happens when it runs dry.

  • Charging car batteries
  • Upkeep

A generator powers the devices in your base. Put fuel in it, start it, and everything of yours within 50 m can run. The more you switch on, the faster it burns fuel.

You need Generator Plans, your own base, and fuel in a jerry can
Reach 50 m, and only your own base’s devices
Tank 40 L, empty when you place it
Fuel burn 0.5 L an hour while running, plus every device that’s switched on
Limit 1 generator per base

Setting one up

  1. Buy Generator Plans from a General Trader, at the Safezone or the Black Market.
  2. Put the plans in your hands and place the generator on the ground inside your base. It can’t go down outside a base.
  3. Fill the tank. A new generator is empty. Hold a jerry can and pour it in at the generator’s fuel cap.
  4. Start it with Start Generator. Only your base’s members get the switch. With nothing in the tank you’ll see Out of fuel.

Checking the fuel

Look at the generator and its prompt shows the tank and whether it’s on, for example Generator - Fuel 45% (Running). Use it for the details: litres left, the current draw in litres an hour, and the hours of running time left at that draw. Anyone can check.

What it powers

A running generator powers every device within 50 m that belongs to the same base. A neighbor’s generator never runs your fridge, even when your fridge is inside its reach. A device with no power refuses with No generator power.

Device Comes from Fuel while on When it’s on
Fridge Fridge Plans 0.5 L/h Always, while it has power
Small Fridge Small Fridge Plans 0.3 L/h Always, while it has power
Sawmill Sawmill 1 L/h Always, while it has power
Cooker or Tiled Cooker Cooker Plans, Tiled Cooker Plans 0.8 L/h Turn Cooker On
Ceiling Light Ceiling Light Plans 0.15 L/h Its light switch
Lamp Table Lamp Plans 0.1 L/h Its light switch
Lamp Lamp, sold boxed 0.15 L/h Its light switch
Free Standing Lamp Free Standing Lamp, sold boxed 0.2 L/h Its light switch
Battery Charger Battery Charger 0.3 L/h Recharge battery charger

General Traders sell every plan and both boxed lamps. Each one is placed from your hands, inside your base.

  • A device that’s switched off burns nothing. Fridges and the sawmill have no off switch, so they burn fuel the whole time the generator runs.
  • Lights need power to switch on. When the power goes they switch off, and they stay off until you switch them on again.
  • Cookers switch themselves off when the power goes. See Cooking and Spoilage.
  • The sawmill won’t process logs without power. See Crafting.

How long a tank lasts

Divide the litres in the tank by the draw. With a full 40 L tank:

Running Draw A full tank lasts
The generator on its own 0.5 L/h 80 hours
Two Fridges 1.5 L/h About 27 hours
A Sawmill and a Fridge 2.0 L/h 20 hours
Two Fridges, a Cooker switched on and four Ceiling Lights 2.9 L/h About 14 hours

When it runs dry

The generator stops by itself when the tank is empty, and everything it powered loses power at once:

  • Lights switch off.
  • Cookers switch off.
  • Fridges stay cold for 30 minutes, or 20 for a Small Fridge. After that, food inside rots at its normal rate. See Cooking and Spoilage.
  • The sawmill stops.

Refill the tank, use Start Generator, then switch your lights and cookers back on.

Floodlights run on their own

A Floodlight, placed from Floodlight Plans, carries its own motor and its own 20 L tank. Your base generator can’t run it.

  • Fill it with a jerry can at its fuel cap. A new one is empty.
  • Its switch starts the motor. With an empty tank it won’t switch on: Out of fuel.
  • It burns 0.6 L an hour while it’s on, so a full tank lasts about 33 hours.

Charging car batteries

A Battery Charger holds enough charge to fill three flat car batteries, and refills from a generator. Traders sell them: see Your First Vehicle.

  1. Put the charger and the battery on the ground, no more than 1.5 m apart. Neither can be in an inventory or a vehicle.
  2. Use Start charging battery on the battery. It gains 20% a minute, so a flat battery is full in 5 minutes. Charging stops when the battery is full or the charger is empty.
  3. Refill the charger on the ground within 50 m of a running generator, with Recharge battery charger. It gains 10% a minute, so it goes from empty to full in 10 minutes.

Upkeep

The generator, fridges, cookers, lights and floodlights all add to your base’s daily upkeep. The UPKEEP tab lists them. See Claiming Your First Base.
