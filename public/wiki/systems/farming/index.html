Farming

Beds and greenhouses, what to plant, when to water, how long crops take, and why you sell the harvest in sacks.

On this page
  • Beds and greenhouses
  • Seeds and water
  • How to grow a crop
  • Grow times and yields
  • Selling in bulk
  • Heroes grow faster, Bandits slower

Farming turns a corner of your base into a steady supply of food and caps. Plant a seed, water it, water it again halfway, and harvest: a greenhouse grows most crops in 20 minutes with at least 4 per plant.

You need A vegetable bed or greenhouse inside your base, seeds or a potato, water
Where Only inside a base you’re allowed to build in
Base limit 4 beds and greenhouses combined
Grow time 20 to 30 minutes in a greenhouse, 30 to 60 in a bed
Waterings Two: once to start, once at 50%

Beds and greenhouses

All three plots are placed like any other base object: put the item in your hands and place it inside your own base’s territory.

Plot Planting spots How to get it
Small Vegetable Bed 2 Craft the kit: 6 Wooden Planks, 6 Nails, 4 Soil (2 minutes)
Large Vegetable Bed 10 Craft the kit: 12 Wooden Planks, 12 Nails, 8 Soil (2 minutes)
Greenhouse 9 Buy Greenhouse Plans from a trader or the black market

A base holds 4 plots in total, beds and greenhouses together. Try to place a fifth and you’ll see Base limit reached: max 4 x Greenhouse. With a greenhouse’s faster growth and bigger harvests, four greenhouses out-produce four Large Vegetable Beds for everything except sheer number of spots.

Moving a plot. With a Building Tool in hand, Packup returns a bed to its kit, and Remove Base Part returns a greenhouse to its plans.

Seeds and water

What you plant

  • Carrot Seeds, Tomato Seeds, Pepper Seeds and Pumpkin Seeds are loot only. No trader sells them. They turn up in Tier 1 to 3 areas, in towns, villages, hunting spots and farms. See How Looting Works.
  • Potatoes are their own seed. Plant a plain Potato. They spawn everywhere and general traders sell them.
  • Soil for bed kits is loot from the same places as seeds.
  • Seeds and Soil don’t wait in loot spots. Untouched ones disappear after about 20 minutes.

Hold the seed you want. The game plants whatever seed is in your hands first. With empty hands it picks a seed from your inventory, which may not be the one you meant. The prompt names the crop before you commit: Plant Carrot.

What you water with

Hold a water container in your hands. Each watering uses part of it:

Container Each watering uses Waterings from full
Watering Can 10% 10
Canteen 20% 5
Water (bottle) 25% 4

The Watering Can is loot from the same places as seeds, and traders and the black market sell it.

How to grow a crop

  1. Plant. Look at an empty spot and use Plant Seeds. You’ll see Crop planted. Water it to start growing.
  2. Water it. With water in hand, use Water Crop. A small plant appears and starts growing.
  3. Wait for 50%. The prompt counts up: Growing… 30%. There’s nothing to do here, and it tells you Crop is still growing…
  4. Water it again. Every crop stops at 50% and waits for a second watering. It won’t finish without one.
  5. Harvest. At 100% the prompt changes to Harvest and the crop name. The crop goes straight into your inventory, and anything that doesn’t fit drops at your feet.

You can see progress from a distance: the plant starts at a tenth of its full size and grows as it matures.

Crops grow whether you’re there or not, and a planted plot survives a server restart.

Grow times and yields

The time is from the first watering to harvest, not counting however long the crop sits at 50% waiting for water. Yields are per plant.

Crop Plant with Vegetable bed Greenhouse
Carrot Carrot Seeds 30 min · 2–8 20 min · 4–8
Tomato Tomato Seeds 30 min · 2–8 20 min · 4–8
Ball Pepper Pepper Seeds 30 min · 2–8 20 min · 4–8
Potato Potato 30 min in a Large bed, 60 min in a Small bed · 2–8 20 min · 4–8
Pumpkin Pumpkin Seeds 60 min · 1 30 min · 1–2

A Pumpkin can be cut up with Slice Pumpkin, which gives 6 Pumpkin Slices, or carved with Craft Pumpkin Face.

Carrots, tomatoes, peppers and potatoes rot in about 24 hours. See Cooking and Spoilage.

Selling in bulk

Sell the harvest in sacks and boxes, not one vegetable at a time. A full sack pays far more than 15 loose vegetables.

Bulk item Made from Time
Carrot Bag 15 Carrots + 1 Empty Burlap Sack 2 minutes
Sack of Potatos 15 Potatoes + 1 Empty Burlap Sack 2 minutes
Fresh Tomatos 15 Tomatoes + 1 Food Crate 2 minutes
Fresh Peppers 15 Ball Peppers + 1 Food Crate 2 minutes
  • Empty Burlap Sack: craft from 1 Cotton Fabric (20 seconds).
  • Food Crate: craft from 4 Wooden Planks and 4 Nails (2 minutes).
  • General traders and the black market both buy all four. Nobody sells them.

Pumpkins have no bulk version.

Heroes grow faster, Bandits slower

Your Humanity standing changes how fast your crops grow and how much you pick. See Heroes and Bandits.

Standing Growth speed Extra items per harvest
Guardian Normal None
Protector 10% faster None
Savior 20% faster +1
Legend 30% faster +1
Outlaw 5% slower None
Raider 15% slower None
Marauder 25% slower None
Warlord 40% slower None
  • Growth speed comes from whoever last planted or watered the crop. Let your group’s highest Hero do the watering.
  • The extra item goes to whoever harvests.
  • After a server restart, a crop grows at the normal rate until someone waters it again.
