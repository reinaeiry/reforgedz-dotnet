Fires and Cooking

Build a Fireplace, light it, cook on it, sit by it to recover, and keep it from vanishing in the wild.

On this page

  • Cooking on it

  • Other fires

A Fireplace costs 5 Sticks and 6 Stone and does three jobs: it cooks your food, warms you up, and gives you somewhere to sit and get your breath back. Just don’t stand in it.

Recipe 5 Sticks, 6 Stone, 30 seconds in the crafting menu
Weight 8 kg
Cooks food Only while lit
Burns you 10 damage a second within about 1.25 m of the centre, while lit
Lasts in the wild 24 hours from when you place it or last light it

Building and lighting a fire

  1. Craft a Fireplace from 5 Sticks and 6 Stone. It takes 30 seconds and weighs 8 kg, so place it close to where you crafted it.
  2. Place it. Put it in your hands and set it on the ground within 5 m of you. It can’t go on a wall.
  3. Light it. Look at the fire and use the light action. It takes 3 seconds and needs no matches or fuel. The same action puts it out.

You can place a Fireplace in the wild or inside your own base. Place one inside someone else’s base territory and it’s removed straight away.

Cooking on it

A lit Fireplace has three prompts:

  • Use Fire (place food) opens its cooking storage. Put your raw food in.
  • Cooking opens a panel showing each item’s stage and progress. It only appears while the fire is lit.
  • Sit Down / Stand Up, covered below.

Nothing cooks on an unlit fire. Food in the storage waits until you light it, and stops cooking the moment the fire goes out.

Take food out once it’s Cooked. It keeps cooking and burns if you leave it in. How long each stage takes, what each stage does to you, and how cooked food keeps are all on Cooking and Spoilage. Fish fillets cook at their own pace: see Fishing.

Don’t stand in the flames

A lit fire hurts anyone standing within about 1.25 m of its centre: 10 damage every second, to random parts of your body. It doesn’t care whose fire it is. An unlit fire is safe to stand in.

Fighting near a fire? Getting pushed into one costs you health fast.

Sitting by the fire

Use Sit Down / Stand Up to sit facing the fire. While you’re sitting:

  • Health comes back 0.3% every 5 seconds, about 3.6% a minute. From nearly dead to full takes close to half an hour.
  • Stamina refills completely every 5 seconds.
  • The fire doesn’t have to be lit to recover, but it does have to be lit to warm you.

A lit fire also warms you and dries your clothes. How much, and from how far, is on Body Temperature.

Why your fire disappeared

A Fireplace in the wild is removed 24 hours after you place it. Lighting it restarts the 24 hours. The clock keeps running while it burns, so light it again (or put it out and relight it) whenever you come back to a camp you want to keep.

A Fireplace placed at your own base becomes part of the base and doesn’t expire.

Tip

Setting up a camp for more than a day? Relight the fire every time you pass through. Each light buys another 24 hours.

Other fires

  • Firepit. Base furniture you craft from 5 Wooden Planks and a jerry can (30 seconds). It can only go inside your base and never expires. You can light it and sit at it the same way, and it burns for only 2 damage a second, but it has no cooking storage.
  • Fires you find already built in the world burn for 10 damage a second and let you sit, but can’t cook.

For a faster, fire-free way to cook, build a cooker at your base: see Cooking and Spoilage.
