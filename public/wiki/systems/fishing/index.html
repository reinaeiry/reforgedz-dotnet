Fishing

Where your float lands decides what can bite. Bait and the time of day decide how soon.

On this page

Fishing turns any stretch of water into food and money. You need a rod, a float and bait, and one button does everything.

You need Crafted Fishing Rod, Fishing Float, bait
Where The ocean, lakes, ponds and rivers. The sea has its own fish.
Control Hold R, or the right trigger on a controller
Best time Bites come about twice as fast 05:00–09:00 and 18:00–22:00, in-game
Cast range Up to 30 m
Sell your catch The Food trader, at the Safezone or the Black Market

What you need

The float and the bait go inside the rod, not on it. Open the rod in your inventory and put them into its storage.

Item How to get it What it does
Crafted Fishing Rod Craft from 3 Sticks, 1 Rope and 2 Metal Scraps (45 s) Holds your float and bait
Fishing Float Craft from 3 Plastic Resin Pellets, 1 Spraypaint Red and 1 Spraypaint White (20 s), or buy one from the Boat Trader You can’t cast without one
Maggot Free: maggots hatch on dead bodies about 10 minutes after death, 4 to 8 per body. The Boat Trader sells them too. Bites come 15% sooner. Used up on a bite.

No bait, no fish. The game lets you cast a bare line, and nothing will ever bite it.

What decides your catch

Which fish can bite

  • The water your float lands in. The ocean and fresh water hold different fish. Brown Trout and Chinook Salmon are the only two found in both.
  • How rare each fish is. Every fish belongs to a tier: common, uncommon, rare or legendary. Each catch rolls a tier first, then picks a fish of that tier that lives in the water your float is in. If that water has no fish of the tier you rolled, you get its best tier instead.
  • Legendary fish follow the clock. They’re most likely at night, next in the golden hours (05:00–09:00 and 18:00–22:00 in-game), and rarest in daylight. The other tiers don’t care what time it is.
  • Luck decides the size. Every fish rolls a weight inside its species’ range. Heavier fish are visibly bigger, weigh more in your pack and sell for more.
  • Where you cast doesn’t matter. Only the kind of water, and the time.

How soon something bites

Effect on the wait
No bait Nothing ever bites
A normal wait 4 to 8 minutes
Maggot 15% sooner
Prime time About twice as fast at its peak, 05:00–09:00 and 18:00–22:00 in-game. It builds over the hour before and fades over the hour after.
Hero fishing perks Up to 20% sooner

What changes nothing

Time of day changes how soon fish bite, and how likely a legendary one is. Weather, depth and how far out you cast don’t change the catch.

How to catch a fish

  1. Load the rod. Put the float and bait into its storage.
  2. Aim. A dot shows where your line will land. White means land, and you can’t cast there. Blue means fishable water.
  3. Hold R to cast, or the right trigger on a controller. Your line reaches up to 30 m.
  4. Wait, and stay close. The line starts creaking when you are about 32 m from your float and snaps at 40 m. While you wait, the game tells you jokes. It won’t repeat one until you’ve heard them all.
  5. Ignore the twitches. Almost half of all nibbles are false alarms: a short, weak tap. Strike at one and you spook whatever was interested, and the next real bite takes longer.
  6. Strike on the real thing. When the float is pulled under you’ll see Bite! Strike now. You get 3 to 4 seconds, whatever the fish. Miss it and there’s about a 1 in 3 chance the fish takes your bait.
  7. Fight it. Hold to reel, let go to let the line recover, and watch the tension bar.
  8. Land it. Once the fish is up at the rod tip, it can’t get away.

The fight

Tension Bar What to do
Under 60% Green Reel
60–85% Amber Ease off
Over 85% Red Let go
100% The line snaps and the fish is gone
  • Every fish makes runs, and you can’t win line during one. Reeling only builds tension, and not reeling gives line back. Keep the bar out of the red and wait for the run to end.
  • Big fish fight longer, about 15% longer per kilogram, up to roughly two and a half times as long.
  • Fish tire. The end of the fight is easier than the start.

What you can catch

Every fish is given a weight when you land it, and the weight goes into its name: Brown Trout (2.4 kg). The ↔ mark means a fish lives in both kinds of water.

After the catch

You can’t eat a whole fish. Drop it, hold a blade and gut it: a knife, machete, katana, tomahawk, axe, bayonet or cleaver all work. The fillets land on the ground around you, not in your pack.

Sell whole, eat the fillets. The Food trader pays more for a whole fish than for its fillets. Gutting is for food, not money.

Don’t leave a catch lying around. Fish on the ground disappear after about 15 minutes.

Cook fillets or they’ll make you sick.

Stage Chance of food poisoning
Raw 85%
Partly Cooked 50%
Half Cooked 20%
Cooked None
Burned 10%

On a fire, a fillet spends about 30 seconds in each stage: about 90 seconds to Cooked, then 30 more before it burns. Raw fillets rot in about 8 hours, and cooked ones last three times as long.

Heroes fish better

On the Hero path, your fishing perks help: waits up to 20% shorter, a strike window up to 30% longer, and faster landing.

How you lose the fish

  • No bait. Nothing bites.
  • Striking at every twitch. Almost half are false alarms, and each one you strike at makes the next real bite slower.
  • Reeling through a run. It builds tension and gains nothing. That’s how lines snap.
  • Wandering off. At 40 m from your float, the line snaps.
  • Filleting to sell. A whole fish is worth more.
