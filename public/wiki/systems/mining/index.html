Mining

Break ore with a Pickaxe at a Mining area and smelt the nuggets at a Furnace. Where to mine on each map, and what each rock gives.

On this page
  • From rock to Iron Bar
  • Where to mine
  • Your own Furnace
  • What it’s for

Iron Bars go into nails, Codelocks, base kits, lockers and barricades, and every one of them starts as ore in a rock. Take a Pickaxe to a Mining area, break the rocks, pick up what falls out, and smelt it at a Furnace.

You need A Pickaxe
Where The Mining areas on your map
You get Iron Nuggets, Gold Nuggets and Stone
Smelting 5 Iron Nuggets make 1 Iron Bar. 10 Gold Nuggets make 1 Gold Ingot.

From rock to Iron Bar

  1. Get a Pickaxe. Craft one at a workbench from 2 Wooden planks and 8 Metal Scrap (30 s), where it’s listed as Pick Axe. Traders sell them too, and they turn up as loot in every tier. It weighs 3.65 kg.
  2. Go to a Mining area. Look for the Mining icon on your map. Iron, gold and stone rocks sit there.
  3. Hit the rock with the Pickaxe. Every heavy swing knocks out one nugget. Light swings work too, but it takes about three of them for each nugget. No other tool or weapon does anything to the rock.
  4. Pick up what falls out. Nuggets land on the ground a couple of metres from the rock. Anything left there disappears after 10 minutes.
  5. Smelt the nuggets at a Furnace. Carry them to it and use Smelt Iron Ore (5 Iron Nuggets into 1 Iron Bar, 10 s) or Smelt Gold Ore (10 Gold Nuggets into 1 Gold Ingot, 25 s).
Rock Gives Heavy swings to empty it What you do with it
Iron ore 10 Iron Nuggets 10 Smelt 5 into an Iron Bar
Gold ore 5 Gold Nuggets 5 Smelt 10 into a Gold Ingot, or use the nuggets
Stone 10 Stone 10 Fireplaces and Bricks
  • A rock shrinks as you mine it and disappears when it’s empty. The damage stays on the rock, so anyone can finish a rock someone else started.
  • Empty rocks don’t come back until the server restarts. Each restart scatters a fresh set across the Mining areas. If an area looks bare, someone got there first.
  • Heroes mine faster. The Hero bonus that speeds up tree cutting works on rock too: 5%, 10%, 15%, then 25% as you rank up. See Heroes and Bandits.
  • Gold is heavy. A Gold Nugget weighs 1 kg. An Iron Nugget weighs 0.1 kg, an Iron Bar 0.5 kg and a Stone 0.5 kg.
  • No trader buys or sells nuggets, bars or ingots. You mine every one yourself.

Where to mine

Chernarus

About 250 iron rocks, 30 gold rocks and 99 stone rocks are placed after each restart.

Mining area What’s there
Zara Quarry Iron, gold and stone. The biggest area.
North-east of Kamenoe Iron, gold and stone
South of Olsha Iron
West of Pisy Iron
West of Kamenka, on the south-west coast Iron, gold and stone
  • Gold also turns up inside Rify’s toxic gas, with no Mining icon. You need a full hazmat set to stand there. See Rify.
  • Stone also turns up at Metalursk, on the west side of the map, with no Mining icon.
  • Every Furnace is on the coast: near Komarovo, Chernogorsk and Elektrozavodsk, north of Solnichniy, and two north-east of Berezino. Most Mining areas are inland or in the north, so plan the trip. See Chernarus.

Faircroft Islands

  • One Mining area, about 150 m west of the Quarry, with iron, gold and stone. After a restart every spot there fills: about 212 iron rocks, 8 gold rocks and 99 stone rocks.
  • Gold is scarce. 8 gold rocks give 40 Gold Nuggets, enough for 4 Gold Ingots on the whole map until the next restart.
  • 2 Furnaces stand on the islands, one beside each of the map’s two Sawmills: near the Powerplant in the west, and about 550 m south of the Safezone. See Faircroft Islands.

Your own Furnace

Traders at the Safezone and the Black Market sell a Furnace. Hold it in your hands to place it at your base, and smelt there instead of carrying ore across the map.

What it’s for

  • Iron Bars: Nails (1 bar makes 10), a Codelock Tier 1, wooden window, door and hatch kits, garage doors, barricades, weapon lockers, Corrugated Metal Sheets, the Well Pump, and the buggy and Mosquito builds.
  • Gold: a Raid Alarm Watch takes 8 Gold Nuggets, and a Raid Siren Kit takes a Gold Ingot. See Raiding and Defending.
  • Stone: a Fireplace takes 6, and Bricks x10 take 20 Stone and 2 Water at a Concrete Mixer.

Every recipe is on Recipes.
