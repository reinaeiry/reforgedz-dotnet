King of the Castle

Bases fight over a castle. The base closest to the flag captures it, waves attack every 5 minutes, and the winner unlocks an airdrop-sized crate.

King of the Castle is a server event where bases fight over a castle. The base with a player closest to the flag captures it, and the first base to 20 minutes of capture wins a locked crate with airdrop-sized loot. Expect players, zombie hordes and bandit squads, all in one place.

Tip

Playing as a group? You must all be members of the same base. There are no groups or squads in this event: your base is your team. Players without a base play solo.

When Rarely, when 40 or more players are online and the next restart is at least 2 hours away
Warning 5 minutes between the announcement and the castle opening
Capture ring 15 m around the flag
To win 20 minutes of capture, or the most progress when time runs out
Waves Every 5 minutes from the first arrival, 5 in all
Time limit 45 minutes for someone to arrive, then 60 minutes from the first arrival
Reward One locked crate, airdrop-sized, open to everyone once the castle is won

When it happens

  • It’s a rare event. It only starts when the server is busy (40 or more players online) and there’s plenty of time before the next restart.
  • When it starts, everyone gets a King of the Castle pop-up and the castle is marked on the map.
  • You get 5 minutes’ warning before the castle opens. Use them to get moving: some castles are a long way off.
  • If nobody reaches the capture ring within 45 minutes, the event ends with nobody arrived.

Teams are bases

Your team is your base. Everyone who owns or is a member of the same base captures together, shares the same progress and wins together.

  1. To play as a group, everyone has to be a member of the same base. At the base flag, the owner or a manager opens the base menu (the action reads Base Managment) and adds you while you stand nearby. See Claiming Your First Base.
  2. Do it before the event. Membership counts the moment it’s set.
  3. A base holds up to 10 players, owner included, and you can only be in one base. That’s the biggest team you can bring.
  4. No base? You play solo, as a team of one.

Warning

Friends who aren’t on the same base count as enemies to each other. They slow each other’s capture down, even if they play in the same squad.

How capturing works

  • The capture ring is the 15 m around the flag in the middle of the castle. Walls and towers outside it don’t count: you have to come down into the open.
  • The base (or solo player) with the player closest to the flag captures.
  • If another base or solo player is in the ring, capture runs at half speed. Kill them or push them out to get full speed back.
  • If zombies or bandits are in the ring, capture pauses until they’re dead.
  • Dead or knocked-out players don’t count, for capturing or for slowing it down.
  • Every base keeps its own progress. If you’re pushed off the flag, you keep what you’ve earned. Retake it and carry on where you left off.

The waves

The castle fights back. When the first player reaches the ring, a timer starts and a wave comes every 5 minutes, whether anyone is holding the flag or not:

  1. 5 minutes: 8 zombies
  2. 10 minutes: a horde of 12 zombies
  3. 15 minutes: 2 bandit squads
  4. 20 minutes: 3 bandit squads, one of them the bandit boss squad
  5. 25 minutes: a zombie boss and 14 zombies

Each wave attacks from 3 or 4 sides at once and goes for the players at the castle. A wave only comes once the one before it is dead, so stragglers you leave alive push the next wave back.

Nobody can win until the last wave has come. A base that gets there early holds at 99% until the zombie boss wave arrives. Survive it, and the castle is yours.

What you see on screen

Within about 150 m of the castle, a King of the Castle progress bar shows:

  • Your base’s progress (You if you’re solo) and the leading base’s progress. A white mark on the bar shows where the leader is.
  • What’s happening: You are capturing - stay closest to the flag, Someone is capturing the flag - eliminate them, Paused - enemies in the ring. Kill them., Holding at 99% - the last wave is coming.
  • The time left before the event ends.

When the flag changes hands and the new base keeps it for 5 seconds, everyone at the castle gets a pop-up:

  • The capturing base: Your base is capturing the castle. Stay closest to the flag and hold it!
  • Everyone else: Someone is capturing the castle flag. Eliminate them!

Names are never shown. You have to find the capturer yourself.

How you win

  • The first base (or solo player) to 100% wins. With a base, every member wins together.
  • If nobody gets there, the event ends 60 minutes after the first player arrived, and the base or solo player with the most progress wins.
  • The winner is announced to the whole server and the crate unlocks.

Danger

When the crate unlocks, anyone can open it. Winning the castle doesn’t make the loot yours: you still have to hold the courtyard while you loot. The crate stays for 15 minutes.

Tips

  • Get your squad onto one base before the event. If you aren’t on the same base, you’re fighting each other.
  • Stay close to the flag. Sitting on a tower captures nothing. The player nearest the flag decides who captures.
  • Clear the ring. One zombie in the ring stops everyone. Kill the waves fast.
  • Don’t give up after a push. Your progress is kept. Retake the flag and keep going.
  • Be ready at 25 minutes. The zombie boss wave is the last one, and nobody wins before it comes.
  • Watch the timer. If time is running out and you’re leading, your job is to stop anyone else catching up.
