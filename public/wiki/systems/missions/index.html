Missions

Hero and Bandit missions, King of the Castle, supply drops, roadblocks, shipwrecks, the patrol helicopter and the things nobody announces.

Missions are events the server starts on its own: guarded camps to clear, crates dropped from the sky, wrecks to loot and a helicopter to shoot down. Most are announced with a pop-up and a map marker. A few are never announced and have to be found.

Mission How you hear about it What you do
Hero / Bandit mission Pop-up and a map marker Kill every guard, then hold the flag for 60 s
King of the Castle Pop-up and a map marker Bases fight to capture a castle
Supply drop Pop-up about 7 minutes ahead, then a map marker Get to the crate first
Roadblock Pop-up only Kill the bandits, then loot the crate
Shipwreck Pop-up and a map marker Loot the wreck
Patrol helicopter Pop-up, repeated every 15 minutes Shoot it down, then loot the crash
Unannounced Nothing Find them

How you hear about missions

  • NEW MISSION pops up for the whole server when an announced mission starts. Most say Check your map for location; the ones that already name a town just say where.
  • MISSION ENGAGED (Hostiles alerted to your presence) and AREA SECURED (Capture the flag to claim rewards) only show for players within 150 m.
  • MISSION COMPLETE and MISSION EXPIRED go to everyone.
  • Missions run on a schedule each restart, at least 15 minutes apart, and announced missions don’t start in the last 30 minutes before a restart. See Server Restarts.

Hero and Bandit missions

The big one. A guarded camp near a town, with a flag in the middle and a locked loot crate.

  • Hero missions are bandit camps (Bandit Outpost, Mission Tower) guarded by bandits.
  • Bandit missions are military checkpoints (Military Checkpoint, Mission Tower) guarded by soldiers, including a sniper.
  • The two take turns. The name tells you which it is, the difficulty and the nearest town: Hero Mission: Bandit Outpost [Hard] near ….

Doing it

  1. Find it. The map marker shows the rough area, about 100 m out. Get within 200 m for 10 s and you engage it: the guards are alerted and the marker moves to the exact spot.
  2. Kill every guard. The flag stays locked until all of them are dead.
  3. Capture the flag. Once it unlocks you see AREA SECURED. Stay within 5 m of the flag for 60 s.
  4. Loot the crate. It unlocks when the flag is captured, and stays for 15 minutes.

Difficulty

Difficulty How often What changes
Easy About 56% Base guards, smallest crate
Normal About 34% Sharper guards, bigger crate
Hard About 8% Much sharper guards, more items and caps
Extreme About 1% The best guards and the biggest crate

Harder missions put more items and more caps in the crate.

Time limits

  • Nobody engages it within 30 minutes: it expires.
  • Everyone leaves: 15 minutes after it was engaged with nobody within 150 m, it’s abandoned.
  • It ends after 60 minutes either way.

Reputation

Capturing the flag pays 500 hero reputation on a Hero mission, or 500 bandit reputation on a Bandit mission, to every player within 150 m. See Heroes and Bandits.

King of the Castle

A rare event where bases fight over a castle. The base with a player closest to the flag captures it, waves attack every 5 minutes, and the winner unlocks an airdrop-sized crate. When the server isn’t busy enough for one, a Hero or Bandit mission runs in its slot. Full rules: King of the Castle.

Supply drops

A helicopter flies a crate in and drops it near a town.

  • About 7 minutes’ warning: … incoming near in about 7 min. Then Supply helicopter heading towards .
  • The crate lands with smoke for 5 minutes and gets a Supply Drop marker.
  • Drops come as building, supply, medical or explosives crates, in three tiers. Higher tiers carry more items and more caps.
  • There are no guards. The danger is everyone else who heard the same pop-up.
  • The crate stays until it’s emptied: Supply drop secured.

Roadblocks

Roadblock spotted near : a bandit roadblock on a road near a town, with a boss among them.

  • There’s no map marker. Use the town in the pop-up and follow the roads.
  • The crate is empty until every bandit is dead, then fills with loot.
  • It’s cleared away 15 minutes after the last bandit dies, and expires after 30 minutes if nobody clears it.

Shipwrecks

Shipwreck Spotted: a wreck washes up on the coast with containers to search. It gets a map marker.

  • No guards.
  • It never expires: the wreck stays until the next restart, or until someone else empties it.

The patrol helicopter

US Army helicopter spotted heading towards : a military helicopter flies a loop over the towns, and the pop-up repeats every 15 minutes while it’s flying.

  • Shoot it down. It takes a lot of damage; small arms alone take a while. When it falls: … shot down near .
  • The crash has 3 to 6 loot cases plus loose loot, and the pilot may drop vehicle keys.
  • A zombie horde guards the crash site.
  • There’s no map marker, so watch where it goes down. The crash stays until the restart.

Things nobody announces

Some events never show a pop-up or a marker. You find them by exploring:

  • A downed airliner comes down somewhere after every restart, with loose loot and a chance of keycards. Its loot lasts 6 hours.
  • Fallen soldiers, bodies left where a fight went wrong, appear after every restart.
  • Small guarded events pop up on roads near towns. Clear the guards and there’s a crate.
  • Bandit patrols roam the roads. Three are always out, and a killed patrol is replaced after 30 minutes.

Tip

Missions draw players. A pop-up tells the whole server where the loot is, so expect company. Clear the guards fast, loot fast and leave.
