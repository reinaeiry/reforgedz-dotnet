Your First Vehicle

Buy or find a vehicle, fit its parts, put your key on it, keep it fuelled and park it in a vehicle terminal.

On this page

  • Why it won’t start
  • Keys and locks
  • Fuel
  • Storing your vehicle
  • Selling a vehicle

A vehicle is yours once your key is on it. Buy one at a trader or fix one up that you find, assign a key straight away, and park it in a vehicle terminal when you log off.

Buy one Vehicle and Heli Traders at the Safezone, the Boat Trader, and military vehicles at the Black Market
To start the engine Every engine part fitted and a battery with at least 5% charge
To drive it All its wheels fitted too
Make it yours Buy a car key and hold Assign Key on the vehicle for 5 s
Keep it safe Store it in a vehicle terminal

Getting a vehicle

Buying one

Trader Sells
Vehicle Trader (Safezone) Cars, vans, trucks and motorbikes
Heli Trader (Safezone, and the Black Market on Faircroft Islands) Helicopters
Boat Trader Boats
Vehicle Trader (Black Market) Military vehicles

When you pick a vehicle, the trader shows its parts. The parts the vehicle comes with are marked FACTORY FITTED. You can swap in a different compatible part before you pay. Your vehicle is delivered with its parts already fitted, onto the delivery pad next to the trader: Vehicle delivered and fitted.

  • Clear the pad first. If anything is parked on it, the purchase stops with Spawn point is blocked - move vehicles away and no caps are taken.
  • A part that runs out mid-purchase is refunded, and the message says how many.

Finding one

Vehicles spawn around the map, but expect parts to be missing. A found car, truck or boat has an empty tank, so bring a jerry can. A found boat never has its spark plug. Open the engine bay and check the wheels before you count on it.

Warning

A vehicle has no key until someone assigns one. A new purchase included. Until your key is on it, anyone can drive it away or put their own key on it. Assign yours before you do anything else.

Why it won’t start

An engine needs every one of its engine parts fitted and working. Wheels don’t stop the engine starting, but you won’t get far without them.

Vehicle Batteries Spark plugs Wheels
Most cars and vans (S105, S1203, Beetle, Mud Beetle, SUV, UAZ, Humvee, M151A2 and others) 1 1 4
Buggy None 1 4
Big trucks (Ural 4320, M923A1) 2 2 6
Transport helicopters (UH-1H, Mi-8, MH-6) 2 4 None
Motorbikes, boats, the Maverick XR and the Mosquito None 1 None

The engine bay

Walk up to the vehicle and use Open engine bay. A header shows how many parts are fitted out of how many it needs, and each empty slot shows a faded picture of the part that belongs there. Drag parts in and out like any other storage.

  • A locked vehicle needs its key before you can open the bay.
  • To change engine parts, the engine must be off, the vehicle stopped and unlocked.

Wheels

Wheels are fitted at the wheel itself: Fit or Remove and the wheel’s name.

  • Hold a repair wrench and carry the wheel, or have it on the ground within 3 m.
  • It takes 30 seconds and the vehicle must be stopped.
  • Wheels aren’t one per model. Several wheel types fit most vehicles, and the prompt names the one you’re about to fit.

The battery

  • It needs at least 5% to start, and every start uses a little more.
  • Driving recharges it by about 5% a minute.
  • It drains while parked. A full battery goes flat in about three days. Lights left on with the engine off drain it about 12 times faster.
  • A battery you find is old stock, holding 20% to 50%. A battery bought from a trader is full.

Spark plugs, car batteries, wheels, repair wrenches, jerry cans and battery chargers are sold by the Vehicle Traders at both hubs, and by the Boat and Heli Traders.

Keys and locks

  1. Buy a car key. The General Trader at both hubs sells them, in black, blue, red and white.
  2. Hold Assign Key on the vehicle for 5 seconds with the key. A key already on another vehicle is refused, and so is a vehicle that already has a key.
  3. Lock and unlock with Lock Vehicle and Unlock Vehicle while you carry the key.
  • Spare keys. At a Drill Press, hold the assigned key and carry an unassigned one, then use Cut Key Copy (10 s). Each key makes at most 3 copies. Drill Presses are built from Drill Press Plans.
  • Upgrade the lock. Upgrade Lock raises it from Tier 1 to Tier 2 for 2,000 caps, then to Tier 3 for 5,000 caps.
  • Lockpicks. A Lockpick can only attempt a Tier 1 lock. Tier 2 and Tier 3 need an Advanced Lockpick. No trader sells either. Both turn up as loot.

Fuel

  • Safe zone pumps charge, and never run dry. On Chernarus and Faircroft Islands the pumps inside the safe zone take caps as the fuel flows, so your balance ticks down while you fill.
  • Pumps out in the world are free, but finite. They start with a little fuel and only refill when players deliver oil. A dry pump stays dry. See The Oil Rigs.
  • Carry a jerry can so a dry pump doesn’t strand you.
  • Heroes pay less at paid pumps, from the second hero rank up.

Storing your vehicle

A vehicle terminal stores your vehicle out of the world, safe from thieves. Safe zones are not car parks: the rules say unattended vehicles there may be deleted.

The terminal in the safe zone is yours alone. It holds land vehicles only, not helicopters.

To store a vehicle, it must be:

  • within 15 m of the terminal,
  • carrying a key that you hold,
  • empty of people, cargo and caps,
  • not destroyed. A damaged vehicle stores fine.

Each refusal names the reason, such as Empty the vehicle before storing it.

  • Storage lasts 7 days per payment. Extend it 7 days at a time, up to 21 days in total.
  • When storage runs out, the vehicle is gone. Extend it before then.
  • You start with 2 slots and can buy more, up to 10.
  • Getting it back: it appears on a nearby parking sign, not at the terminal. If every spot is taken you’ll see All your parking spots are occupied. Clear one and try again.
  • Heroes pay lower garage fees from the second hero rank up.

Base terminals work the same way for a whole group. See Claiming Your First Base.

Selling a vehicle

Only the Black Market buys vehicles, and only military vehicles, trucks, vans and motorbikes. Cars, boats and helicopters can’t be sold back. Park on the trader’s parking spots, carry the key, get everyone out, and make sure every part is fitted. See Traders and Caps.
