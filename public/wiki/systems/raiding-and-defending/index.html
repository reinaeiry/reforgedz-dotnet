Raiding and Defending

Walls can't be broken, so every raid goes through a lock. What breaks locks, what warns you when yours are hit, and how to make a raid cost more.

On this page
  • The rules
  • Raiding a base
  • Defending your base

You can’t blow a hole in a wall. Walls, floors, foundations, roofs, doors, storage and defences take no damage from anything. Every raid goes through a lock: break it with C4 and whatever it guarded is open.

What breaks Codelocks, and the built-in locks on Codelocked doors, hatches, garage doors, gates and storage
What breaks it C4
What doesn’t break Every other base piece
Warning for the owners Raid Siren, Raid Alarm Watch
Allowed? Yes. The server rules encourage it.

The rules

The Base Rules panel in every flag’s base menu says it plainly: raiding is allowed and encouraged, glitches and exploits during a raid are not, and only approved raid tools and explosives may be used. Two more rules cover helicopters. Owners may not build around a helicopter to stop it being lockpicked, and raiders may not blow up a helicopter at a base unless they’re really raiding that base. The full list is on Server Rules.

Raiding a base

What you need

C4. Craft a C4 Block from 2 Gunpowder Tin, 2 Sulphur and 1 Electrical Parts (5 minutes).

Lockpicks won’t help. They open vehicles, never codelocks.

Read the lock first

Look at any lock and the readout tells you what it’ll take: HP: 400/400 (C4 to destroy: 4). The casing shows the tier too, since each tier looks different.

Lock Tier 1 Tier 2 Tier 3
Codelock fitted to a door or box 4 C4 6 C4 8 C4
Built into a Codelocked part 2 C4 4 C4 6 C4

A built-in lock only takes damage while it’s locked. If it’s already unlocked, walk in.

Breaking in

  1. Plant the C4 on the lock, or right next to it. Each charge goes into the lock it’s planted on, or the nearest one.
  2. Watch the readout drop by one charge per blast.
  3. When it hits zero:
    • A fitted Codelock is destroyed. The door or box it was on opens for anyone.
    • A built-in lock breaks. The part stays, but it no longer keeps anyone out until the owners repair it.

Warning

If the owners have built a Raid Siren or carry a Raid Alarm Watch, they know the moment your first charge hits a lock. Expect company.

Abandoned bases

A base whose upkeep runs out is abandoned: every lock opens, the whole server gets an ABANDONED BASE banner, and an Abandoned Base marker goes on the map. No C4 needed. It stays open for at least 4 hours, then it is deleted. See Claiming Your First Base.

Defending your base

Know when you’re hit

Both alarms go off when C4 hits a lock in your base.

Alarm How to get it What it does
Raid Siren Craft a Raid Siren Kit: 12 Iron Bars, 4 Nuts & Bolts, 1 Electrical Parts, 1 Gold Ingot (4 minutes). Place it inside your base circle. A siren sounds for 1 minute. Every blast restarts it.
Raid Alarm Watch Craft it from a wristwatch, 1 Electrical Parts and 8 Gold Nuggets (2 minutes) Will play a beep if your base is breached. It beeps for 30 seconds on every member online who carries one, wherever they are.

The watch beep is quiet and only heard close to it, so it wakes you, not the raiders.

Make every door cost more

  • Upgrade your locks. Tier 2 costs 2,000 caps and Tier 3 a further 5,000. A Tier 3 fitted Codelock takes 8 C4. See Codelocks.
  • Fit a Codelock rather than relying on a built-in lock. At every tier, a fitted Codelock takes 2 more C4 than a built-in lock.
  • Put locks behind locks. Walls can’t be breached, so raiders pay for every locked door between them and your loot. An airlock of two locked doors doubles the bill.
  • Lock the boxes too. A locked safe or crate inside a breached room is another lock to break.
  • Set a code on every lock. A lock with no code set doesn’t lock anything.

Defences

Defences shape where raiders can walk and stand, and like every base piece they can’t be destroyed.

  • Sandbags: Sandbag Wall, Sandbag Round Wall, Sandbag Window Wall, Sandbag Tall Wall and the Sandbag Bunker.
  • Barricades: the Hedgehog Barricade and the Barbed Fence Barricade.
  • Camo nets: the Camo Net Wall.
  • Barbed garage doors: Left and Right Large Garage Door (Barbed), both with Codelocked versions.

Recipes are on the Recipes page.

After a raid

  • Repair built-in locks with an Electrical Toolbox: Repair Lock (consumes Toolkit). A repaired lock is back at Tier 1 with no code, so set a code and upgrade it again.
  • Replace destroyed Codelocks. A fitted Codelock that was blown off is gone, and so is its tier. Fit a new one, set a code and upgrade it again.
  • Change your codes if anyone might have seen one.
  • Keep the wallet funded. An abandoned base needs no C4 at all.
