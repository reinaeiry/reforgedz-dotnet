Your First Hour

A tick-box checklist from your first spawn to minute 60, starting with what you carry and what to do first.

On this page
  • Before you spawn
  • Minutes 0 to 5: take stock
  • Minutes 5 to 20: leave town with something useful
  • Minutes 20 to 40: water, and staying out of trouble
  • Minutes 40 to 60: plan your exit
  • Your first week

The server keeps running while you are offline. You will lose your first characters. This page gets you through one hour.

You spawn At a random spawn point. On Chernarus that is somewhere on the south or east coast. On Faircroft Islands it is in the west, around Arlo Tower and Boot Lagoon.
Food and water 50% each
Clothes T-shirt, jeans and sneakers
You carry An Apple, a bottle of Water, a Field Dressing, a WW2 Bike and a folded paper map
Weapon None

That is the Neutral kit. Heroes and Bandits spawn in different clothes, and every Hero and Bandit kit adds a flashlight. See Heroes and Bandits.

Before you spawn

  • [ ] Pick a look. A new character chooses from seven appearance groups. The Spawn button unlocks after 5 seconds.
  • [ ] Coming back? If your last character is still alive, you skip this screen and wake up where you logged out, with everything you had.

Minutes 0 to 5: take stock

  • [ ] Drink twice. The bottle holds 4 drinks of 25% water each, so two drinks take you from 50% to full. A drink doesn’t land all at once: it tops you up over about 50 seconds.
  • [ ] Keep the empty bottle. You can refill it at a well pump.
  • [ ] Eat the Apple. Two bites, 10% food each.
  • [ ] Remember the 80% rule. Your health only comes back on its own while food and water are both at 80% or more.
  • [ ] Deploy the bike. Hold the WW2 Bike and press R, or the right trigger on a controller. Scroll to turn the preview, then press R again to place it on open, flat ground. The Dismantle Bike action packs it back into an item in 2 seconds.
  • [ ] Find yourself on the map. On Chernarus you start in or near Chernogorsk, Elektrozavodsk, Kamyshovo, Solnichniy or Berezino, or somewhere else along the south and east coast.

Minutes 5 to 20: leave town with something useful

  • [ ] Keep quiet. Zombies are drawn to gunfire and noise, and every spawn town on Chernarus has them. A punch from an ordinary zombie takes 15% off the body part it hits, and 22.5% at night. See Zombies.
  • [ ] Search every container you pass. Loot is rolled when you search, and a failed roll opens the container empty. See How Looting Works.
  • [ ] Grab a blade, a backpack, more water and bandages before anything else.
  • [ ] Pick up a tin opener if you see one. Canned food has to be opened with a Tin Opener: drag the can onto it in your inventory, or put the can on the ground and use the opener there. See Food and Water.
  • [ ] Bandage bleeding straight away. You have exactly one Field Dressing. See Wounds and Bleeding.

Minutes 20 to 40: water, and staying out of trouble

  • [ ] Find a well pump. It offers Drink Well Water, Fill water bottle and Wash Hands.
  • [ ] Stay out of military sites. Soldiers patrol them and they are not friendly. You have no gun yet.
  • [ ] Don’t start fights with other players. The server rules protect fresh spawns unless they start the fight. See Server Rules.
  • [ ] Cook meat and fish before you eat them. A raw fish fillet poisons you 85% of the time, and you feel it 2 to 7 minutes later. See Cooking and Spoilage.

Minutes 40 to 60: plan your exit

  • [ ] Watch your water. Standing still, it drops 1% every 2 minutes. Every minute of sprinting costs another 1.2%. The first warning, I’m feeling thirsty…, comes at 20%.
  • [ ] Stay warm. Spend 25 minutes with a body temperature below 32 °C and you can start getting sick. See Body Temperature.
  • [ ] Check the clock. The server restarts at 00:00, 04:00, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00 and 20:00 UTC, with warnings 30, 10, 5, 2 and 1 minute before. See Server Restarts.
  • [ ] Log out properly. The Exit button unlocks after 30 seconds. Inside a safe zone it reads Leave SafeZone and stays locked, so walk out first. Never quit while unconscious: you come back dead, on a new character.
  • [ ] If you die, move fast. Your body stays lootable for 30 minutes, and a restart removes it sooner. If a zombie killed you, a zombie wearing your clothes and carrying your gear stands up 5 minutes later, where your body came to rest. See Death and Combat Logging.

Your first week

  • [ ] Fishing: food and money from any stretch of water.
  • [ ] The Safezone and Traders and Caps: where to sell what you find.
  • [ ] Crafting and Salvage: turn junk into materials and tools.
  • [ ] Claiming Your First Base and Codelocks: somewhere to keep it.
  • [ ] Your First Vehicle: something faster than the bike.
