Controls

The keys ReforgedZ adds to Arma Reforger, for keyboard and controller, grouped by what you're doing.

On this page

ReforgedZ adds its own keys for moving, using what’s in your hands, sorting your pack, building, trading and lockpicking. Most keys not on this page work as they do in vanilla Arma Reforger. Controller buttons use Xbox names.

The on-screen hints at the edge of your screen show the right key when an action is available, so if you forget one, look there first.

On foot

Action Keyboard and mouse Controller
Autorun on or off Right Ctrl Hold RB, press X
Hold your breath while aiming Hold Left Shift Hold right stick click
Punch Left mouse button RT
Block Hold middle mouse button Hold LT
Tread water on or off X Left stick click
  • Autorun stops by itself when you push forward or open the map. Press the key again to stop it yourself.
  • Hold Breath only appears when you’re aiming down the sights of a weapon.
  • Punching needs empty hands, and it doesn’t work inside a safe zone.
  • Blocking costs stamina. Without enough stamina you can’t start a block.
  • Treading water needs fins. The hint reads Tread Water while you swim with fins on, and Stop Treading once you are.

What’s in your hands

One key uses whatever you’re holding. The hint names what it will do.

Holding Hint Keyboard Controller
Food or a drink Consume Food/Drink R RT
A painkiller Take Painkiller R RT
A medkit Treat Yourself R RT
A fishing rod Cast, then Reel in Hold R Hold RT

Fishing has its own guide: see Fishing.

Inventory

These work while your inventory is open.

Action Keyboard and mouse Controller
Rotate the item R Right stick click
Auto-sort the storage you’re pointing at T Left stick click
Split a stack, choosing how many Middle-click Menu
Split a stack in half Hold middle mouse button Hold Menu
Drop the item on the ground G No controller key
  • Middle-click a backpack, vest or other container to open it. Containers never split.
  • Put down what you’re carrying first. Auto-sort and drop won’t work while an item is on your cursor.
  • On a controller, D-pad up and down skip empty rows inside an open container, jumping to the nearest row with an item in it.

Building

Take the building tool in your hands, then open build mode with B, or the right trigger on a controller. V also switches build mode on and off.

Action Keyboard and mouse Controller
Piece menu B X
Place R RT
Rotate Mouse wheel RB + D-pad left or right
Rotate 90° Left Shift + mouse wheel RB + D-pad left or right
Raise or lower Left Ctrl + mouse wheel RB + D-pad up or down
Pitch Home / End LB + D-pad up or down
Roll Insert / Delete LB + D-pad left or right
Snap on or off T Right stick click
Cancel, or leave build mode Right mouse button B

The hint shows whether snapping is on: Snapmode : ON or Snapmode : OFF. For what to build and where, see Claiming Your First Base.

Placing codelocks and moving objects

Action Keyboard and mouse Controller
Rotate Mouse wheel RB + D-pad left or right
Rotate 90° (kits and plans only) Left Shift + mouse wheel RB + D-pad left or right
Height Left Ctrl + mouse wheel RB + D-pad up or down
Place R RT for a codelock, X when moving an object
Cancel a move Esc B

More on locks in Codelocks.

The weapon filter only works on the ammo and attachment tabs, and clearing only works while you’re selling. That’s why both can share the Menu button on a controller. Trading is covered in Traders and Caps.

Lockpicking

Action Keyboard and mouse Controller
Aim the pick Move the mouse: the pick points at your cursor Left stick left or right turns it. Let go and it stays put.
Apply pressure Hold F or the left mouse button Hold RT
Fine aim No keyboard key Hold LB while turning
Give up Esc B

Changing a key

Open the game’s Controls settings. ReforgedZ adds a ReforgedZ group (Autorun, Hold Breath, Tread Water) and a Building group (Rotate Placement). The use key for what’s in your hands, R by default, is under Equipment.
