How You Die in Your First Hour

The mistakes that kill new players, sorted by how early they catch you, with the number behind each one.

On this page
  • In the first ten minutes
  • In the first half hour
  • In the first hour
  • After you die: how you lose your kit

Most first deaths come from the same short list of mistakes. They are sorted here by how soon each one tends to catch you.

In the first ten minutes

  • Don’t fight zombies in your spawn town. Every spawn town on Chernarus has zombies, and they are drawn to noise and gunfire, so one fight pulls in the next. A punch from an ordinary zombie takes 15% off the body part it lands on. Zombies
  • Bandage bleeding the moment you see it. The game’s own loading tip puts it plainly: bleeding will kill you. You spawn with exactly 1 Field Dressing, so find more before your second wound. Wounds and Bleeding
  • Stay out of military sites. Soldiers patrol them and shoot at you. Your spawn kit has no weapon to answer with. Where to Go for What

In the first half hour

  • Don’t fight zombies after dark. Zombie punches hit half as hard again at night: 22.5% off the body part they land on. Zombies
  • Never eat raw meat. A raw fish fillet poisons you 85% of the time, and the poisoning shows up 2 to 7 minutes after you eat, so it’s easy to blame something else. Cooking and Spoilage
  • Drink before you sprint. You spawn at 50% water. Standing still costs 1% every 2 minutes, and every minute of sprinting costs another 1.2%. Sprint everywhere and that 50% is gone in about 30 minutes. At 0% you take damage every 2 seconds until you drink. Food and Water

In the first hour

  • Eat and drink up before you get hurt. Your health only comes back on its own while food and water are both at 80% or more. You spawn at 50% of each, so a wound taken in your first hour stays with you until you fix it or fill up. Staying Alive
  • Get warm before you get sick. Spend 25 minutes with a body temperature below 32 °C, or above 40 °C, and sickness can start. Body Temperature · Sickness and Medicine
  • Stay out of toxic zones. A Toxic Zone icon on your map marks each one. Standing inside the toxic zone poisons you every second. Only a full hazmat set, both the head and the body, keeps it out. Sickness and Medicine
  • Never log out while unconscious. You come back dead, on a new character, instead of waking up where you fell. The Exit button takes 30 seconds to unlock anyway, so quitting is never a quick escape. Death and Combat Logging

After you die: how you lose your kit

  • Get back to your body within 30 minutes. A body stays lootable for 30 minutes, and a server restart removes it sooner. Restarts come every 4 hours, at 00:00, 04:00, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00 and 20:00 UTC. Server Restarts
  • Hunt the zombie that killed you. 5 minutes after a zombie kills you, a zombie stands up where your body came to rest, wearing your clothes and carrying your gear. If a zombie is still feeding on your body, it waits until the meal is over. Kill it to get your kit back. Zombies
