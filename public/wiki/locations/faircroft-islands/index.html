Faircroft Islands

The ReforgedZ map on EU2 and NA2. Which regions have the better loot, where the services are, and what will kill you on the way.

On this page
  • Loot tiers by region
  • Services
  • Getting around
  • The oil rig and the aircraft carrier
  • Dangers

Faircroft Islands runs on two servers: EU2 and NA2. You spawn in the west, where the loot is weakest, and it gets better once you leave it for the north, the middle, the east or the south coast.

The islands are the work of Lakes Dan, and ReforgedZ is lucky to call them home. Special thanks to Lakes Dan for the map.

Servers EU2, NA2
You spawn In the west, around Arlo Tower and Boot Lagoon
Tier 3 loot RAF Haverbrook, the ground around the Brickworks, the oil rig and the aircraft carrier
Safe trading The Safezone, halfway between Mertun and Seahaven
Unsafe trading The Black Market, at South Huts on the south coast
Only on this map A Boat Trader inside the Safezone, and a Heli Trader and a Boat Trader at the Black Market

Loot tiers by region

Every item can only spawn in certain tiers, so a Tier 2 rifle never turns up in a Tier 1 town. Where zones overlap, the higher tier wins. How tiers work is on How Looting Works.

Tier Region Towns and sites in it
Tier 1 The west Calverston, Parkside, Broughton, Tanmuth, Chipping Stratton, Arlo Tower, Bay View Farm, Hill Top Farm, Round Island, the Refinery, the Recycling Centre
Tier 2 The rest of the islands Pendleworth, North Island Port, Fenby, Sandwick, Crennan, Calaig, Faircroft Aerodrome, Mertun, Brockway, Ogden, the Quarry, La’l Venice, Fellside, Seahaven, Cairnrigg, Shaak Island, Tarren-on-Sea, St Margaret’s Rock, South Huts
Tier 3 Small pockets RAF Haverbrook and the ground east of it, the ground around the Brickworks, the oil rig, the aircraft carrier
  • Every spawn point is in Tier 1. You start with the weakest loot on the map.
  • The south coast is Tier 2. Tarren-on-Sea and St Margaret’s Rock sit below the Tier 1 towns but roll Tier 2.
  • The aircraft carrier is Tier 3. It lies about 2.3 km north-east of Cairnrigg.
  • Most of the Brickworks pocket is inside the toxic zone. See Dangers below.
  • The Tier 3 pockets are small. Expect other players there.

Services

Trade

  • The Safezone has 9 traders, with the Boat Trader on its waterfront. The Broker hands out Contracts there, and it has one vehicle terminal. Nobody can hurt you inside. It sits about 550 m north of the eastern Sawmill, 1.6 km north-east of Mertun and 1.7 km west of Seahaven.
  • The Black Market at South Huts has 9 traders, including a Heli Trader and a Boat Trader. It sells heavier weapons, armor and military vehicles, and it buys vehicles. Nobody is protected there.
  • The two hubs are about 5.8 km apart in a straight line, with the Black Market to the south.

Stations on your map

  • 6 wells carry the Well icon: at Ogden, Broughton, Crennan and Calaig, beside the Black Market at South Huts, and by the Fire Station near Pendleworth. Use Drink Well Water at the pump. See Food and Water.
  • 1 Mining area, labelled Mining, about 150 m west of the Quarry. See Mining.
  • 2 Furnaces and 2 Salvage Stations. A Furnace and a Salvage Station stand together beside each of the map’s two Sawmills: one near the Powerplant in the west, and one about 550 m south of the Safezone. See Crafting and Salvage.
  • A Toxic Zone icon marks the ground about 250 m west of the Brickworks. The gas there is real. See Dangers below.

Fuel and oil

  • Safezone pumps charge caps and never run dry.
  • Pumps out in the world are free. They start with a little fuel, then hold only what players deliver. 4 station tankers take oil: by the Fire Station near Pendleworth, at the Refinery, by the Hospital in Calverston and at Deluxe Petrol. See The Oil Rigs.

Getting around

  • Spawn area. You start at one of 43 spawn points, picked at random, all in the west: around Arlo Tower, Boot Lagoon, Bay View Farm and Hill Top Farm, near Round Island and Chipping Stratton, north of Hill Foot Farm, and one near the Refinery. See Your First Hour.
  • Union Bridge is marked just north of the spawn coast, by the Channel, on the way to North Island Port and Pendleworth.
  • Boats spawn at 8 spots: North Island Port, the harbor by the Scrap Yard near Pendleworth, Crennan, Calaig, the coast east of Sandwick, St Margaret’s Rock, and two near RAF Haverbrook.
  • Land vehicles spawn in towns, at farms and industrial sites, at RAF Haverbrook and Faircroft Aerodrome, and at the Calverston Police Station.
  • Vehicles and boats you find spawn with an empty tank. Find fuel before you count on one. See Your First Vehicle.
  • The trade hubs are a long walk. From the spawn area, the Safezone is about 4.1 km east and the Black Market about 6.2 km south.

The oil rig and the aircraft carrier

  • The oil rig stands out at sea about 3.2 km north of Seahaven, and 4 km from the Safezone. It is Tier 3.
  • The aircraft carrier lies about 2.3 km north-east of Cairnrigg. It is Tier 3.

Both are marked on your map with their own icons, Oil Rig and Aircraft Carrier.

Getting aboard, oil runs, keycards and crate loot are on The Oil Rigs.

Dangers

  • Zombies in the towns. They’re placed in Calverston (including its Hospital, Police Station, Fire Station and Building Site), Tanmuth, Broughton, Tarren-on-Sea, the Refinery, the western Sawmill, Chipping Stratton, Pendleworth, the Processing Plant, North Island Port, Mulligan’s Farm, Crennan, Calaig, Ogden, the Quarry, Mertun, Seahaven and Fellside. See Zombies.
  • Zombies at the airfields and the Brickworks. They’re also placed at RAF Haverbrook, Faircroft Aerodrome and the Brickworks.
  • Toxic gas at the Brickworks. The Toxic Zone icon marks it, and the Brickworks itself is inside. Standing inside the toxic zone poisons you every second. Only a full hazmat set, both the head and the body, keeps it out. Where to Go for What lists the gear.
  • Roaming bandits. Bandit patrols move around the map with no map marker. A wiped patrol is replaced about 30 minutes later.
  • Military sites. Soldiers guard them, and they shoot. Don’t walk in without a gun and a plan.
  • Other players. Expect them at the Tier 3 pockets, the oil rig and on the way out of both trade hubs.
  • Restarts. The server restarts at fixed times with warnings. See Server Restarts.

Bandit camps are hidden in the far west and in the east. None is on your map.

Military held sites include an island in the far north-east, with a bunker. Old checkpoints with loot stand in the south and the north.

Drug processing is set up in the south-west, beside the Drug Dealer icon. No dealer stands there at the moment, so nothing buys drugs. See Drugs.
