The Oil Rigs

Every map has an offshore oil rig and an aircraft carrier. Tier 3 loot on the rigs, and oil runs that pay you and refill the map's fuel pumps.

On this page

  • Oil runs
  • On the rig
  • The aircraft carrier
  • Dangers

Every map has one offshore oil rig and one aircraft carrier, and both stay in the same place every restart. The rig is a Tier 3 loot zone and the only place oil comes from: pump it into an Oil Can, bring it to a fuel station, and you get paid while the station’s pumps fill up.

Oil rig Aircraft carrier
Chernarus (EU1, NA1) Off the north-east coast, under 1 km east of Rify Off the south-west coast, about 1.3 km south of Kamenka
Faircroft Islands (EU2, NA2) Out at sea, about 3.2 km north of Seahaven At sea, about 2.3 km north-east of Cairnrigg
Loot tier Tier 3 on every map Tier 3 on every map

Both are marked on your map, with their own Oil Rig and Aircraft Carrier icons.

Getting there

  • Take a boat. Boats spawn along the coasts, and the Boat Trader sells them. See Your First Vehicle.
  • The rig has a pier. Come alongside and take the stairs up to the decks.
  • The carrier is boarded by ladder. Ladders run up its hull from the water.
  • Put your key on the boat first. A boat with no key on it can be driven away by whoever reaches it next, and you’ll be stranded on the rig.

Oil runs

Oil is how the fuel pumps out in the world get refilled. A pump nobody has used starts each restart with 250 L, and a station tanker nobody has used starts with 1,250 L. After that, only oil fills them.

  1. Get an Oil Can. Craft one from 1 Jerry Can and 1 Spraypaint Red in 1 minute (see Recipes), or find one in towns, villages, industrial sites, farms and on the coast. It holds 20 L.
  2. Fuel the rig pump. The pump on the rig’s well head has its own 40 L tank and burns at least 2 L an hour while it runs. Hold a jerry can and pour fuel in at its fuel cap. Rig Pump shows the tank level.
  3. Start it. Use Start Rig Pump. With an empty tank you’ll see Out of fuel. Anyone can start or stop it.
  4. Fill the can. Hold the Oil Can in your hands and use Extract oil at the tap. The label climbs as it fills, Extract oil (45%), and a can takes 20 seconds. If the pump is off you’ll see The rig generator is not running.
  5. Sell it at a fuel station, one of two ways:
    • Station tanker. Some fuel stations have a tanker beside the pumps. Hold the can and use Deliver oil. It empties 2 L a second and pays as it pours. A full station shows This station is full.
    • Any fuel pump. Set the can down within 6 m of a pump and use Empty into pump. The label tells you the litres and the caps before you commit: Empty into pump - 20 L for … caps.

Both ways pay the same per litre, and a part-full can pays for what it holds.

What your oil does

Where you sell it The pumps gain
A station tanker 14 L of fuel for every 20 L of oil, shared by every pump on that forecourt
A pump out in the world 14 L of fuel for every 20 L of oil
A pump in the Safezone Nothing. Those pumps never run dry, so it’s a straight sale.

Oil isn’t fuel. An Oil Can says Cannot be used as vehicle fuel. and can’t be poured into a vehicle.

On the rig

  • Tier 3 loot lies across the decks, so Tier 3 items can turn up here. See How Looting Works.

  • Keycards can spawn here, Level 01 to Level 04. What they open is on Keys and Locked Containers.

  • Contracts send you here. Some ask you to reach the rig or the carrier. See Contracts.

  • Soldiers guard the rig on both maps.

The aircraft carrier

  • The Blue Key crate is in the carrier’s tower, on both maps. See Keys and Locked Containers.
  • Weapon crates and gear crates are aboard. Each search succeeds 50% of the time, and each crate resets after 6 minutes or 1 hour, depending on the crate.
  • Loose loot spawns aboard: weapons, clothing, food and explosives.
  • Tier 3 on both maps.

Dangers

  • You’re a sitting target at the tap. Extracting takes 20 seconds a can, with the can in your hands and your weapon lowered.
  • Anyone can switch the pump off, or burn the fuel you poured in.
  • One way off. Lose your boat and you’re swimming.
  • A toxic zone next door on Chernarus. The Rify wreck, west of the rig, lies inside one. Standing inside the toxic zone poisons you every second. Only a full hazmat set, both the head and the body, keeps it out.
  • Other players. Everyone knows where the rig and the carrier are, and Tier 3 loot draws them.
