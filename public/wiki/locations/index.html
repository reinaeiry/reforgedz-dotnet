Locations

Where to go for what, and what you will find when you get there.

  • Where to Go for WhatA task index. Find what you need, see where to go on each map, and follow the link for how it works.
  • ChernarusThe ReforgedZ map on EU1 and NA1. Which regions have the better loot, where the services are, and what will kill you on the way.
  • Faircroft IslandsThe ReforgedZ map on EU2 and NA2. Which regions have the better loot, where the services are, and what will kill you on the way.
  • The SafezoneWhere combat switches off. Trade, store your vehicle and pick up contracts, then walk out with 30 seconds of protection.
  • The Black MarketThe trade hub with no protection. Heavier weapons, armour, military vehicles, and the only place that buys vehicles.
  • RifyThe wrecked cargo ship on Chernarus's north-east coast. Tier 3 loot, keycard spawns, hard crates and toxic gas that only full hazmat keeps out.
  • The Oil RigsEvery map has an offshore oil rig and an aircraft carrier. Tier 3 loot on the rigs, and oil runs that pay you and refill the map's fuel pumps.
