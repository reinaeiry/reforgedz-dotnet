The Safezone

Where combat switches off. Trade, store your vehicle and pick up contracts, then walk out with 30 seconds of protection.

On this page
  • Where it is
  • The rules inside
  • Traders
  • The Broker
  • Your vehicle

The Safezone is the one place on the map where nobody can hurt you. It’s where most of the traders are, where you store your vehicle and where you pick up contracts. Look for the Safezone icon on your map.

Inside You can’t be hurt, and nobody can shoot, throw or punch
After you leave 30 more seconds of protection
Vehicles inside Can’t be damaged
Looting None. Containers can’t be searched and nothing spawns
Also here Traders, a vehicle terminal, paid fuel and The Broker

Where it is

Map Where
Chernarus (EU1, NA1) On Green Mountain, north-east of Zelenogorsk
Faircroft Islands (EU2, NA2) About 550 m north of the eastern Sawmill, between Mertun and Seahaven

The rules inside

Combat is switched off. When you walk in you see SAFE ZONE: Combat disabled.

  • You can’t take damage.
  • You can’t fire, throw a grenade or punch. The buttons do nothing.
  • Vehicles inside can’t be damaged. Their protection ends the moment they drive out.
  • No looting. Containers show No Looting Zone, and cash registers can’t be searched. See How Looting Works.
  • Sickness can’t build here. See Sickness and Medicine.
  • Third-person view works on foot. Everywhere else, on foot, the game keeps you in first person.

Leaving

Your protection lasts 30 seconds after you step out. You see You left safe zone. Protection ends in 30s, with reminders at 20, 10 and 5 seconds, then SAFE ZONE: Protection ended.

  • You can’t shoot during those 30 seconds either. The countdown protects you and keeps you harmless.
  • Step back in and the countdown stops.

Warning

Expect someone to be watching the way out. Leave with a plan, not a full bag and no idea where you’re going.

Logging out

The Exit button reads Leave SafeZone and stays locked while you’re inside. Walk out, wait for the normal logout timer, then quit. See Death and Combat Logging.

Server rules

  • No killing, looting, vehicle theft or camping around safe zones.
  • Safe zones are not garages. Unattended vehicles may be deleted.
  • No building within 400 m.

See Server Rules.

Traders

Every trader deals only in its own goods. How buying, selling, tax and reputation work is on Traders and Caps.

Trader Deals in
Weapon Trader Guns, ammunition and attachments
Clothing Trader Clothes, headwear, gloves, backpacks and night vision
Medical Trader Medicine and medical supplies
Food Food and drink. Buys fish.
General Trader Tools, melee weapons, car keys and base-building plans. Buys collectables.
Vehicle Trader Cars, vans, trucks, motorbikes and vehicle parts
Heli Trader Helicopters and vehicle parts
Boat Trader Boats, fishing gear and vehicle parts
Money Exchange Caps notes at face value, no tax

Which map has which

  • Chernarus: every trader above except the Boat Trader. The Boat Trader has its own spot on the east coast, about 800 m south-east of Tulga, with its own map icon. It isn’t inside a safe zone, so you have no protection there.
  • Faircroft Islands: all nine, with the Boat Trader on the waterfront inside the safe zone.

The Broker

The Broker sits in the Safezone on Chernarus and Faircroft Islands and hands out daily and weekly contracts. See Contracts.

Your vehicle

The vehicle terminal stores a land vehicle for you alone, and your vehicle comes back out onto a parking sign nearby. Chernarus has two terminals side by side, and Faircroft Islands has one. Helicopters can’t be stored here.

Pumps in the Safezone charge caps and never run dry, on Chernarus and Faircroft Islands. Heroes from the second hero rank up pay less.

The full walkthrough is on Your First Vehicle.
