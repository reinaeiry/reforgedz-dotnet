Where to Go for What

A task index. Find what you need, see where to go on each map, and follow the link for how it works.

On this page
  • At a glance
  • Icons to look for
  • What your map won’t show
  • By map

Find what you need in the left column. Everything named here is marked on your in-game map or is a place you can’t miss. Anything the map doesn’t mark, you find for yourself.

At a glance

You want Go to Differs by map
Water A well pump: Drink Well Water or Fill water bottle. See Food and Water. Faircroft Islands marks its wells with the Well icon
A first rifle Towns, villages and hunting spots in Tier 1 and Tier 2 areas. The SKS and the Mosin-Nagant spawn there. Every other rifle, and every shotgun and machine gun, is a military find
A pistol Towns, villages and police stations
Sell loot The traders in the Safezone. The Food trader buys fish, and the General Trader buys collectables. See Traders and Caps. Vehicles only sell at the Black Market
Buy a vehicle The Vehicle and Heli Traders in the Safezone, a Boat Trader, or military vehicles at the Black Market Chernarus: the Boat Trader is on the east coast, outside the Safezone. Faircroft Islands: a Boat Trader in the Safezone, and a Heli Trader and a Boat Trader at the Black Market.
Store a vehicle The vehicle terminal in the Safezone. See Your First Vehicle. Chernarus has two, Faircroft Islands one
Take a contract The Broker, in the Safezone. See Contracts. Both maps
Smelt ore A Furnace, or place your own. Mine the ore first at a Mining area. See Mining. Chernarus: 6 Furnaces, 5 Mining areas. Faircroft Islands: 2 Furnaces, 1 Mining area.
Salvage A Salvage Station, or build your own. See Salvage. Chernarus: 4 stations. Faircroft Islands: 2.
Craft A Crafting Workbench, or place your own. See Crafting.
Fish Any water. The sea holds different fish from lakes and rivers. See Fishing. The Boat Trader sells fishing gear. The Food trader buys fish.
Fuel Safezone pumps charge caps and never run dry. Pumps out in the world are free, start with a little fuel, and after that only hold what players deliver. Both maps have Safezone pumps
Caps Sell fish to the Food trader, run oil from the oil rig, or take contracts. See The Oil Rigs.
Better loot Tier 3 areas. See How Looting Works. Chernarus: Rify, the oil rig and the carrier. Faircroft Islands: its Tier 3 areas, the oil rig and the aircraft carrier.
A keycard Rify and the oil rig have keycard spawns, and a few more spots in Tier 2 and Tier 3 areas can roll one. See Keys and Locked Containers. Rify is Chernarus only
Hazmat gear A Gas Mask from the Clothing Trader at the Black Market, and an NBC Suit. The Black Market stocks suits, but starts empty after a wipe until players sell some in, and two more spawn as rare Tier 3 military finds on each map. You need both to stand in a toxic zone. See Rify. Chernarus: Rify. Faircroft Islands: the Toxic Zone west of the Brickworks.

Icons to look for

Well, Furnace, Mining, Salvage Station, Crafting Workbench, Safezone, Black Market, Boat Trader, Toxic Zone, Oil Rig and Aircraft Carrier. Every icon is on Map Icons.

What your map won’t show

  • Bandit camps. They exist, they’re garrisoned, and they aren’t on your map.
  • Military sites. Soldiers guard them. Stay out until you have a gun and a reason.
  • Roaming bandits. Patrols move around the map with no marker.

By map

  • Chernarus (EU1, NA1): loot tiers by region, every station and the dangers are on Chernarus.
  • Faircroft Islands (EU2, NA2): loot tiers by region, the wells, both trade hubs and the dangers are on Faircroft Islands.
  • Both maps have a Safezone, an offshore oil rig and an aircraft carrier. See The Safezone and The Oil Rigs.
