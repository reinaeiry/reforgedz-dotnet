The Black Market

The trade hub with no protection. Heavier weapons, armour, military vehicles, and the only place that buys vehicles.

On this page
  • Where it is
  • How it differs from the Safezone
  • Traders
  • Dangers nearby

The Black Market is the second trade hub, and the dangerous one. There’s no protection, so anyone can shoot you while you trade. In return it stocks what the Safezone won’t sell, and it’s the only place that buys vehicles.

Protection None. Combat works normally.
Looting None right around the traders
Only here Explosives, most armour vests, military vehicles, the flash drive
Buys vehicles Military vehicles, trucks, vans and motorbikes
On the map A Black Market icon

Where it is

Map Where
Chernarus (EU1, NA1) In the far north-west corner, high in the woods west of Pisy
Faircroft Islands (EU2, NA2) On the south coast, at South Huts

How it differs from the Safezone

  • Nobody is protected. You can be killed while the trade menu is open. The Safezone’s 30-second shield doesn’t exist here.
  • It buys vehicles. The Black Market Vehicle Trader takes military vehicles, trucks, vans and motorbikes. No trader anywhere buys back a car, boat or helicopter.
  • Different stock. The Black Market’s weapon, clothing, general and vehicle traders sell a different range from their Safezone twins.
  • Same rules for money. Tax, stock and your reputation work the same way at both hubs. See Traders and Caps.

What you can only buy here

  • Explosives.
  • More guns, ammunition and attachments, including extra sniper rifles and machine guns.
  • Armour vests. Almost every vest is sold here and not at the Safezone.
  • Military vehicles, from the Humvee to the Ural and M923A1 trucks.
  • The flash drive.

What only the Safezone sells

Cars, vans, trucks, motorbikes and most everyday clothing, eyewear and gloves. On Chernarus, helicopters too.

Traders

Trader Deals in
Weapon Trader Guns, ammunition, attachments and explosives
Clothing Trader Clothes, headwear, armour vests, backpacks and night vision
General Trader Tools, melee weapons, car keys, base-building plans and the flash drive. Buys collectables.
Vehicle Trader Military vehicles and vehicle parts. Buys military vehicles, trucks, vans and motorbikes.
Medical Trader Medicine and medical supplies
Food Food and drink. Buys fish.
Money Exchange Caps notes at face value, no tax
Heli Trader Helicopters and vehicle parts
Boat Trader Boats, fishing gear and vehicle parts
  • Chernarus: the first seven. No Heli or Boat Trader.
  • Faircroft Islands: all nine.

The medical, food, money, heli and boat traders here carry the same range as the Safezone’s.

Drugs sell here. Cannabis Buds, Bagged Cannabis and Cannabis Bricks sell at the Black Market on both maps, and nowhere else. Faircroft Islands also has a Drug Dealer icon beside its drug scales, but no dealer stands there. See Drugs.

Dangers nearby

  • Other players. Everyone here is carrying caps or about to be. Expect watchers on the approach roads and on the high ground.
  • Chernarus: the corner is remote and a long way from the Safezone on Green Mountain. Bring fuel and a way home.
  • No building close by. Rule 13 bans building within 400 m of the Black Market. See Server Rules.

Tip

Sell first, then buy, then leave by a different road from the one you came in on.
