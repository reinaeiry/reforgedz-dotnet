Chernarus

The ReforgedZ map on EU1 and NA1. Which regions have the better loot, where the services are, and what will kill you on the way.

On this page
  • Loot tiers by region
  • Services
  • Getting around
  • Dangers

Chernarus runs on two servers: EU1 and NA1. You spawn on the south and east coasts, where the loot is weakest, and it gets better the further you head north-west or out to sea.

Servers EU1, NA1
You spawn On the south or east coast
Tier 3 loot Rify, the oil rig and the carrier
Safe trading The Safezone, on Green Mountain north-east of Zelenogorsk
Unsafe trading The Black Market, in the far north-west, west of Pisy
Only on this map Rify

Loot tiers by region

Every item can only spawn in certain tiers, so a Tier 2 rifle never turns up in a Tier 1 town. How tiers work is on How Looting Works.

Tier Region Towns and sites in it
Tier 1 The south coast and the east Balota, Komarovo, Chernogorsk, Elektrozavodsk, Kamyshovo, Solnichniy, Nizhnoye, Berezino, Khelm, Msta, Dolina, Orlovets
Tier 2 The west, the centre and the north Kamenka, Pavlovo, Zelenogorsk, Vybor, Grishino, Lopatino, Pustoshka, Stary Sobor, Novy Sobor, Staroye, Gorka, Polana, Gvozdno, Krasnostav, Northwest Airfield
Tier 3 Small pockets Rify, the oil rig and the aircraft carrier
  • Kamenka is the exception on the coast. The far south-west corner belongs to Tier 2, not Tier 1.
  • The Tier 3 pockets are small. Expect other players there.

Services

Trade

  • The Safezone has the traders, The Broker for Contracts, two vehicle terminals and paid fuel. Nobody can hurt you inside.
  • The Black Market sells heavier weapons, armor and military vehicles, and buys vehicles. Nobody is protected there.
  • The Boat Trader stands on the east coast, 800 m south-east of Tulga, with its own map icon. It sells boats and fishing gear, and it isn’t in a safe zone.

Stations on your map

  • 6 Furnaces, all on the coast: near Komarovo, Chernogorsk and Elektrozavodsk, north of Solnichniy, and two on the coast north-east of Berezino. See Crafting.
  • 4 Salvage Stations: near Zelenogorsk, Gorka and Berezino, and one in the north-west, east of Sangheilistok. See Salvage.
  • 5 Mining areas, labelled Mining: at Zara Quarry, north-east of Kamenoe, south of Olsha, west of Pisy, and on the south-west coast west of Kamenka. See Mining.

The oil rig and the aircraft carrier have their own map icons, Oil Rig and Aircraft Carrier.

Fuel and oil

  • Safezone pumps charge caps and never run dry.
  • Pumps out in the world are free. They start with a little fuel, then hold only what players deliver. 21 fuel stations have a station tanker that takes oil for the whole forecourt. See The Oil Rigs.

Getting around

  • Spawn towns. You start in or near Chernogorsk, Elektrozavodsk, Kamyshovo, Solnichniy or Berezino, or elsewhere on the south and east coast. See Your First Hour.
  • Boats spawn along the south and east coasts: around Kamenka, Komarovo, Balota, Chernogorsk, Elektrozavodsk, Kamyshovo, Solnichniy, Nizhnoye and Berezino. Expect missing parts and an empty tank.
  • The trade hubs are in the west. From the east coast, the Safezone and the Black Market are a long trip. A bike or a car pays for itself. See Your First Vehicle.

Dangers

  • Zombies in every major town and at both airfields. A punch takes 15% off the body part it hits, and 1.5 times that at night. See Zombies.
  • Roaming bandits. Bandit patrols move around the map with no map marker. A wiped patrol is replaced about 30 minutes later.
  • Toxic gas at Rify. Standing inside the toxic zone poisons you every second. Only a full hazmat set, both the head and the body, keeps it out.
  • Military sites. Soldiers guard them, and they shoot. Don’t walk in without a gun and a plan.
  • Other players. Rify, the oil rig and the trade hubs draw the most.
  • Restarts. The server restarts at fixed times with warnings. See Server Restarts.

Bandit camps are hidden in the north, the centre and inland in the east. None is on your map.

Military held sites are in the far north-west, inland in the north-east and on the south coast.
