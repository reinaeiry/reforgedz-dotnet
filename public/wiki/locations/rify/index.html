Rify

The wrecked cargo ship on Chernarus's north-east coast. Tier 3 loot, keycard spawns, hard crates and toxic gas that only full hazmat keeps out.

On this page
  • What’s there
  • The toxic zone
  • Getting there
  • What to bring
  • Dangers

Rify is the wrecked cargo ship run aground on Chernarus’s north-east coast. The wreck and the shore around it are a Tier 3 loot zone, the best tier any part of the map gives, and it’s one of the few places with spawns set aside for keycards.

Map Chernarus only (EU1, NA1)
Where The north-east coast, about 1.5 km east of Khelm and 2 km north-east of Berezino
Loot tier Tier 3 on the wreck and its shore
On your map The Toxic Zone icon
Toxic gas Yes. Wear a full hazmat set
Rare finds Keycards, Level 01 to Level 04
Nearby The oil rig, under 1 km out to sea

What’s there

Loose loot

Loot lying on the wreck and the shore rolls from the Tier 3 pool, so items that never appear in Tier 1 or Tier 2 areas can turn up here. How Looting Works explains tiers.

  • The lighthouse isn’t part of the zone. It stands just outside, in Tier 1.
  • 12 spots on and around the wreck spawn only keycards, Level 01 to Level 04. Keycards are rare everywhere, so don’t count on one every visit. One left on the ground lasts 4 hours.

What a keycard opens is on Keys and Locked Containers.

Crates

Six crates sit on and beside the wreck.

  • A search succeeds 30% to 50% of the time, depending on the crate.
  • A crate resets 30 minutes to 1 hour after it’s searched.

A failed search still starts the wait, so one bad roll can close a crate for an hour.

The toxic zone

Look for the Toxic Zone icon on your map: that’s Rify. It’s the only one on Chernarus, and the gas is real.

Danger

Standing inside the toxic zone poisons you every second. Only a full hazmat set, both the head and the body, keeps it out.

The gas covers the wreck and the shore around it, about 300 by 270 m. The keycard spots and all six crates are inside it.

  • Head: a Gas Mask. The Clothing Trader at the Black Market sells it. It’s also a rare find at industrial and medical spots in Tier 2 and Tier 3 areas.
  • Body: an NBC Suit, yellow or green. It’s a rare military find in Tier 3 areas. The Black Market only has one in stock after a player sells it there.
  • Half a set does nothing. A Gas Mask without the suit, or the suit without a mask, leaves you poisoned.
  • Step out and it stops. The poison ends within a second of leaving the zone.

Getting there

  • Head for the north-east coast. Rify is past Berezino, on the shore east of Khelm.
  • By boat. Boats spawn along the south and east coasts, including near Berezino and Nizhnoye, a short run from the wreck. Expect a found boat to be missing parts and to have an empty tank. See Your First Vehicle.
  • Stop in Berezino on the way. The Berezino area has two Furnaces and a Salvage Station, all marked on your map.

What to bring

  • A Gas Mask and an NBC Suit. Wear both before you step onto the shore.
  • Room in your pack. The point of the trip is to leave heavier than you came.
  • Bandages and a splint. See Wounds and Bleeding.
  • A weapon. Tier 3 is a small slice of the map, and other players want it too.
  • A plan for keycards. They only pay off at a bunker, so know which one you’re taking a card to before you leave.

Dangers

  • The gas. Without a full hazmat set, the wreck and its shore poison you every second.

  • Other players. Everyone on the server sees the same icon on their map, and the good loot sits on a small patch of coast.

  • A long way home. The Safezone on Green Mountain is more than 11 km away, on the other side of the map.

  • Bandit patrols. Roaming bandit patrols move around Chernarus with no map marker, and a wiped patrol is replaced about 30 minutes later.

  • Zombies. No zombies are set to spawn at the wreck itself. The nearest spawns are in the villages inland, more than 1 km away. See Zombies.

  • A bandit camp. One is hidden inland in the east of the map, with no map marker.
