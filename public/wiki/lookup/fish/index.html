Fish

Every fish in ReforgedZ, where it lives, how rare it is and how big it gets.

On this page
  • By water
  • Every fish

This list comes straight from the game’s own data, so it changes when the mod does. For how to catch them, see Fishing.

Where your float lands decides which of these can bite. Water types that hold the same fish share one table.

Every fish

Barracuda

All teeth and bad temper. Hits the bait like a thrown knife and does not let go. Good eating if it came from clean water.

Found in Ocean
Rarity Rare
Weight 1.5–12 kg
Fillets 1–7

Benigoi Koi

Solid deep red from nose to tail, no markings at all. A pond fish that went feral and kept its colour.

Found in Lakes, ponds and rivers
Rarity Uncommon
Weight 1.2–7 kg
Fillets 1–6

Black Buffalo

A slab of a fish that sat on the bottom getting older and heavier while everything above it fell apart. Some of these were swimming before your grandparents were born.

Found in Lakes, ponds and rivers
Rarity Legendary
Weight 8–30 kg
Fillets 1–10

Bluegill

A palm-sized panfish from the still water. Nobody brags about one, but they bite all day and they fry up fine.

Found in Lakes, ponds and rivers
Rarity Common
Weight 100–800 g
Fillets 1–2

Brown Trout

Speckled, hard-fighting and best eaten the same day. The rivers are full of them if you know which pools to work.

Found in Ocean, Lakes, ponds and rivers
Rarity Common
Weight 0.4–4 kg
Fillets 1–5

Chinook Salmon

A king salmon, and it fights like one. Deep red meat, feeds a group, and the traders know exactly what it is worth.

Found in Ocean, Lakes, ponds and rivers
Rarity Uncommon
Weight 2.5–18 kg
Fillets 1–8

Clownfish

Bright orange, barely a mouthful, and absolutely nobody's dinner. Someone with a tank will pay stupid money for it.

Found in Ocean
Rarity Rare
Weight 50–350 g
Fillets 0–1

Common Carp

A heavy bottom-feeder that will eat almost anything you put down. Muddy tasting, but there is a lot of it and it never stops biting.

Found in Lakes, ponds and rivers
Rarity Common
Weight 1.5–9 kg
Fillets 1–6

Elephantfish

Odd-looking thing with a snout it uses to root through the silt. Bony, but the traders pay for the novelty.

Found in Lakes, ponds and rivers
Rarity Uncommon
Weight 0.3–2.5 kg
Fillets 1–3

Karasugoi Koi

Almost black, and near invisible in deep water until it turns. The old keepers called it the crow carp.

Found in Lakes, ponds and rivers
Rarity Uncommon
Weight 1.2–7.5 kg
Fillets 1–6

Koi Carp

An ornamental carp gone feral in the inland water. Heavy, slow, and worth far more to a collector than to a cook.

Found in Lakes, ponds and rivers
Rarity Uncommon
Weight 1.2–7 kg
Fillets 1–6

Mackerel

Oily, fast, and it never comes up alone. Not worth much each but you rarely land just the one.

Found in Ocean
Rarity Common
Weight 0.3–2 kg
Fillets 1–3

Northern Pike

All jaw and bad intentions. It will take a lure meant for something half its size and then try to take your hand as well.

Found in Lakes, ponds and rivers
Rarity Rare
Weight 2–15 kg
Fillets 1–7

Orange Carp

A gold-scaled carp that escaped somebody's ornamental pond and got big. Same muddy meat as the common sort, but it turns heads at the trader.

Found in Lakes, ponds and rivers
Rarity Uncommon
Weight 2–10 kg
Fillets 1–6

Platinum Ogon Koi

Solid metallic white, and unmistakable even underwater. Before all this, one of these changed hands for more than a car.

Found in Lakes, ponds and rivers
Rarity Legendary
Weight 2–9 kg
Fillets 1–6

Red Snapper

Deep-water fish with a hard mouth, so set the hook properly or lose it. Worth the trip out past the shallows.

Found in Ocean
Rarity Uncommon
Weight 0.8–8 kg
Fillets 1–6

Sardine

A common coastal fish. Not much of a meal on its own, but the traders always take them.

Found in Ocean
Rarity Common
Weight 0.2–1.2 kg
Fillets 1–3

Sea Bass

Firm white meat and no wasted weight. Works the surf line at dusk, which is exactly when you should not be standing in it.

Found in Ocean
Rarity Common
Weight 0.8–6 kg
Fillets 1–5

Sea Turtle

Came up on the line like a paving slab with flippers. Protected once, back when there was somebody left to do the protecting.

Found in Ocean
Rarity Legendary
Weight 20–50 kg
Fillets 1–14

Showa Sanshoku Koi

Black, red and white wrapped around each other with no two alike. Show stock, once. Now it is just a big fish in a dead pond.

Found in Lakes, ponds and rivers
Rarity Rare
Weight 1.4–8 kg
Fillets 1–6

Stingray

Came up off the bottom like a wet doormat and fought like one too. A lot of meat on the wings, if you know where to cut.

Found in Ocean
Rarity Rare
Weight 5–25 kg
Fillets 1–8

Taisho Sanshoku Koi

White ground with red plates and a scatter of black. Bred for a hundred years to look like this and it still does.

Found in Lakes, ponds and rivers
Rarity Rare
Weight 1.4–8 kg
Fillets 1–6

Tancho Koi

Pure white with a single red spot on the crown, and the collectors will not shut up about how rare that is. They are not wrong.

Found in Lakes, ponds and rivers
Rarity Rare
Weight 1.2–6.5 kg
Fillets 1–6

Yamabuki Ogon Koi

Metallic gold the whole length of it. Catches the light from three metres down, which is exactly how you spot one.

Found in Lakes, ponds and rivers
Rarity Rare
Weight 1.5–8.5 kg
Fillets 1–6
