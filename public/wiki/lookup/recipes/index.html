Recipes

Every crafting recipe in ReforgedZ, with ingredients, output and time, grouped by where it is crafted.

On this page

Every recipe players can craft, grouped by where it is made. Names are the in-game names. A recipe marked x10 makes 10 items per craft. Times are for one craft; workbench times change with humanity. How crafting works is on the Crafting page.

Unpack: a Box of 20 Nails unpacks into 20 Nail in 2 s.
