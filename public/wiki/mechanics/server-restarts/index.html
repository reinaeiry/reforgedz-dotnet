Server Restarts

When ReforgedZ restarts, the warnings you get beforehand, and what is still there when the server comes back.

On this page
  • The schedule
  • The warnings
  • What’s still there after a restart
  • What’s gone
  • Before a restart

Every ReforgedZ server restarts every 4 hours: 00:00, 04:00, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00 and 20:00 UTC. Your character, your base and your stashes come through a restart. Bodies on the ground don’t.

Schedule Every 4 hours: 00:00, 04:00, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00, 20:00 UTC
Warnings 30, 10, 5, 2 and 1 minutes before
Still there Your character and gear, reputation, bases, stashes, crops, vehicles
Gone Dead bodies and everything on them

The schedule

Restarts run on the same clock on every server and every map. The times are UTC, so convert them to your own time zone once and remember them.

The warnings

Each warning is an orange SERVER RESTART banner that every player on the server sees. It stays up for 8 seconds and plays a sound.

  1. SERVER RESTART in 30 minutes!
  2. SERVER RESTART in 10 minutes!
  3. SERVER RESTART in 5 minutes!
  4. SERVER RESTART in 2 minutes!
  5. SERVER RESTART in 1 minute!
  6. Server is restarting now. Please reconnect shortly. The server closes a few seconds later.

Two reminders run between restarts:

  • Once an hour the same banner shows the schedule: Server restarts at 00:00, 04:00, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00, 20:00 UTC. The hour is counted from when the server came up, not from the top of the hour.
  • Every 30 minutes a tip appears in chat, marked [ReforgedZ]. It’s picked at random, and one of them is the restart schedule.

If a server comes up close to its next restart, it skips the warnings that are already past.

What’s still there after a restart

  • Your character. You reconnect where you were, with your gear, hunger and thirst as they were.
  • Your reputation and stats.
  • Your base: flags, built pieces, codelocks with their codes, and what’s stored inside.
  • Your stashes.
  • Crops growing in garden plots.
  • Vehicles, with their keys, locks, parts and what’s in them.
  • Marketplace listings, and anything waiting for you to claim.
  • Trader stock.

What’s gone

  • Dead bodies, and everything on them. A body normally lasts 30 minutes. A restart removes it early, whoever it belonged to. See Death and Combat Logging.
  • Opened bunkers reset. A bunker that’s been opened reads Bunker Already Looted until the next restart, and then it can be opened again.

A restart doesn’t look after things you’ve neglected. An in-game tip warns that an unused vehicle gets cleaned up. A base whose flag wallet runs dry is abandoned, and later deleted. See Claiming Your First Base.

Before a restart

  • Don’t start a body run you can’t finish. If the restart lands first, your body is gone.
  • Finish your fight before the last warning. A body lying on the ground when the server closes won’t be there when it comes back.
  • Check the clock before a long trip. Six restarts a day means one is never more than 4 hours away.
