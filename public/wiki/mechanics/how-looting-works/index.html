How Looting Works

Loot tiers, why a container comes up empty, what Search and Open mean, and how long before loot comes back.

On this page

  • Searching a container
  • Loot lying in the world
  • Where you can’t loot
  • Cash registers

Nothing is in a container until you search it. Each search rolls a chance of success, and win or lose, that container is done for about 30 minutes. Better loot sits in higher-tier parts of the map.

Search chance Household furniture fails more often than military crates. Military crates succeed 30–50% of the time, and the aircraft carrier’s crates 50%.
Container resets after About 30 minutes
Loot lying in the world Gone after about an hour if nobody takes it
Tiers on both maps Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3
No looting Safe zones and black markets

Loot tiers

Both maps are split into loot tier zones, and every item can only spawn in certain tiers. A Tier 2 item never turns up in a Tier 1 area, however long you look. Where zones overlap, the higher tier wins.

Items are also limited to certain kinds of place, so what’s there depends on both the tier and the spot.

Map Tier 1 Tier 2 Tier 3

Faircroft Islands (EU2, NA2) The west: Calverston, Broughton, Tanmuth, Chipping Stratton and Arlo Tower Most of the islands, including Pendleworth, Calaig, Mertun, Ogden, Seahaven, South Huts and the aircraft carrier RAF Haverbrook, the Brickworks and the oil rig. Town by town on Faircroft Islands.

Tier 3 is always a small slice of the map, so expect a fight for it.

Each map also has one Tier 3 military held site: in the far north-west of Chernarus, and in the north-east of Faircroft Islands.

Searching a container

Look at a container and the prompt tells you its state:

Prompt What it means
Search Nobody has searched it since it last reset. Your search rolls for loot.
Open Someone has already searched it. You can open it and take what’s left, but nothing new is rolled.
Searched A one-time find that’s already been taken this restart
Locked / Unlock Needs a coloured key in your hands. See Keys and Locked Containers.
Bunker Doors Closed Bunker crates stay shut until the bunker is opened. See Keys and Locked Containers.

What happens when you search

  1. Hold the search. Furniture and wooden crates take 1 second. Military crates and locked containers take 5.
  2. The game rolls. Household furniture fails more often than military crates. Military crates, like the ones at Rify, succeed 30% to 50% of the time, and the aircraft carrier’s crates 50%. On a success, loot appears inside and the container opens.
  3. On a failure it opens empty. That’s the roll, not someone beating you to it.
  4. Either way, the container is done. The prompt now reads Open for everyone. It rolls nothing new until it resets.

When a container resets

About 30 minutes after it was searched, a container resets. Anything still inside is removed and the prompt goes back to Search. After a failed search the prompt keeps reading Open, but the container can roll again once the 30 minutes are up. Military crates reset on their own timer instead: 6 minutes, 30 minutes or 1 hour, depending on the crate.

  • Take what you want the first time. Leftovers don’t wait for you.
  • An empty Open container isn’t worth a second look. Come back after it resets.
  • A failed search still costs the full wait. There’s no retry.

Loot lying in the world

Some loot spawns loose, on floors and shelves, not inside containers.

  • Untouched loot disappears after about an hour, give or take 15 minutes.
  • Once someone takes an item, it’s about 30 minutes before the world spawns another one of it.
  • Some items vanish faster: seeds and Soil after about 20 minutes. See Farming.

Where you can’t loot

Safe zones and black markets are no-loot areas. Try to search a container there and you’ll see No Looting Zone. See The Safezone and The Black Market.

Cash registers

Cash registers stand at some traders and a few other spots. Use Search Register:

  • 25% chance of 100 to 600 caps, straight into your inventory (Found 350 caps!).
  • Otherwise you’ll see The register is empty.
  • Either way, you’re locked out of that register for 30 minutes. Other players aren’t: the wait is yours alone.
  • Not inside a safe zone. The action is blocked there.

Tip

Search every container on your route once and move on. A building’s containers all reset on their own clock, so a loop through a town pays better than waiting at one house.
