Death and Combat Logging

What you keep and lose when you die, how you respawn, what you spawn with, and why logging out while downed kills you.

On this page

  • Your body
  • What you keep
  • What you spawn with
  • Stuck under the ground
  • Logging out

When you die you lose everything you were carrying, and you keep your reputation. Your body stays where you fell for 30 minutes with all your gear on it, unless a server restart comes first.

Death screen 5 seconds, then character select
Spawn button Unlocks after 5 seconds
Your body Lootable for 30 minutes. A restart removes it.
You keep Your reputation, and anything that wasn’t on your body
Log out while downed You come back dead, as a fresh character
Exit button Unlocks 30 seconds after you open the pause menu

What happens when you die

  1. The death screen covers your view for 5 seconds.
  2. Character select opens. Pick a look. The Spawn button counts down, Spawn (5), Spawn (4), and unlocks after 5 seconds.
  3. You spawn at a random spawn point, wearing and carrying the starting kit for your reputation rank.

Your death counts toward your stats. If that life was your longest, it becomes your record.

Your body

  • Everything you carried stays on your body, where it fell, for anyone to loot.
  • It lasts 30 minutes, then it disappears with whatever is still on it.
  • A server restart removes it early. If the next restart is close, a body run may not be worth it. See Server Restarts.
  • Leaving the game doesn’t take your body with you. It stays where it fell whether you’re online or not.

Killed by a zombie? Your body gets up. Five minutes after a zombie kills you, a zombie stands up where your body came to rest, wearing your clothes and carrying your gear, and your body is gone. Kill it to get your kit back. If a zombie is eating your body when the five minutes are up, it waits until the meal is over. See Zombies.

What you keep

  • Your reputation. Dying doesn’t reset it, so your next spawn kit still matches your rank.
  • Anything that wasn’t on your body: what’s stored in your base, your stashes and your vehicles.

What you spawn with

Your kit depends on your reputation rank when you spawn. Every rank gets a folded paper map, a WW2 Bike and at least one field dressing.

Rank Clothes Items
Neutral T-shirt, blue jeans, sneakers Apple, water, field dressing, WW2 Bike, paper map
Guardian, Protector, Savior Combat shirt and trousers (camo, then green, then plain), jungle boots As Neutral, plus a flashlight
Legend Jacket, grey jeans, black cowboy boots, cowboy hat, leather gloves, rucksack Baked Beans, Canteen, Tin Opener, field dressing, flashlight, WW2 Bike, paper map
Outlaw, Raider, Marauder Bomber jacket (plain, then red, then black), jeans, sneakers As Neutral, plus a flashlight
Warlord Camouflage jacket and trousers, jungle boots, camo-net neck gaiter, leather gloves Meatballs, Redneck Beer, 2 field dressings, Tin Opener, flashlight, WW2 Bike, paper map

The rank that counts is the one you hold at the moment you spawn, so a rank change shows up on your next life.

Stuck under the ground

If you fall through the map, open the pause menu. An Unstick Me button appears whenever you’re under the terrain, and pressing it puts you back on the surface with Unstuck!. The server also checks on its own and lifts out anyone it finds under the map, so you may be put back before you reach the menu.

Logging out

The Exit button makes you wait

Open the pause menu and the Exit button reads Please Wait - 30 seconds and counts down. After 30 seconds it becomes Exit Server. The countdown starts again every time you open the menu, so close it and you start over.

Inside a safe zone the button reads Leave SafeZone instead, and stays locked.

Logging out while downed kills you

Being downed means you’ve been knocked unconscious. If you leave the game while you’re downed, you come back dead. You return to character select and spawn as a fresh character with the starting kit. Your downed body stays where it lay, with all your gear on it, for anyone to loot.

It doesn’t matter why you left. Quitting, the game crashing and a dropped connection all count.

Danger

A downed player who disconnects loses their gear, the same as if they’d been killed. If you’re down, wait it out or wait for help.

Combat logging is against the rules

Leaving the game to escape a fight is combat logging, and the server rules forbid it. The loading screen puts it plainly: Do not combat log. Wait for the death screen before logging out. See Server Rules.
