Zombies

A zombie punch wounds the body part it hits. How zombies find you, what their hits do, and what gets up if one kills you.

A zombie punch takes a fixed slice off the body part it lands on, and ordinary medical kit patches it up. Block, be careful at night, and don’t go down near one: five minutes after a zombie kills you, a zombie wearing your gear gets up.

What a hit does

Day Night
Ordinary zombie punch 15% of that body part’s health 22.5%
Punch you block 1.5% 2.25%
  • It’s an ordinary wound. Bandages, IFAKs, morphine and splints treat it like any other injury. See Wounds and Bleeding.
  • Zombies swing slowly. An ordinary zombie swings no faster than once every 1.5 seconds.
  • A hit can make you ill. A solid hit has a 1 in 10 chance to add 8% sickness. See Sickness and Medicine.
  • Don’t get knocked out. Once you’re unconscious, zombies stop punching and can start feeding on you, and their bites aren’t held to 15%.
  • Heroes take less. At Protector you take 10% less damage from zombies, at Savior 20%, and at Legend 30%. Bandits take 8% less at Marauder and 15% less at Warlord.

Block

Hold Middle Mouse, or the left trigger on a controller. Blocking cuts a zombie’s punch by 90%. You need some stamina to start blocking, and holding the block drains it, so block the swing and hit back in the gap before the next one.

How they find you

  • They see you and hear you. Movement in the open gets you spotted.
  • Gunshots carry. A shot draws zombies from well outside the fight. A suppressed weapon is heard over a shorter distance and alerts fewer zombies.
  • They scream for help. A zombie that spots you screams, and zombies within 140 m of it that aren’t already chasing someone come running.
  • Reputation hides you. Zombies spot a Savior 10% closer than normal, a Legend 15% closer, and a Warlord 20% closer. Their screams carry less far too: 113 m for a Savior, 101 m for a Legend and 90 m for a Warlord.
  • Distance loses them. A zombie drops the chase once you’re more than 200 m away.

Doors, vehicles and each other

  • Zombies can’t open doors, so they break them. A door is safe only while it has a code lock with a code set and locked. An unlocked code lock doesn’t stop them. More zombies on one door break it faster, up to three zombies’ worth. See Codelocks.
  • They can’t climb ladders.
  • Vehicles protect you. Zombies can’t open vehicle doors or climb in. A zombie that reaches you in a vehicle beats on the vehicle, not on you.
  • They can’t hurt each other. You can’t bait them into fighting.
  • They feed. Zombies stop to eat fresh bodies, players and AI alike.

Types, bosses and respawns

Most zombies are townsfolk. Soldiers, firefighters, medics and nuns turn up too, and Faircroft Islands adds police, nurses, hazmat workers, clowns, priests and monks.

  • Bosses are rare. A boss zombie’s hits aren’t held to 15%, it can swing every 1.2 seconds, and it takes three to six times as much killing.
  • Kills stay dead. Zombies you kill don’t come back when you leave and return.
  • Wiped groups come back later. Kill a whole group and a fresh one spawns in its place: after an hour at most spots, sooner at a few, the quickest after 5 minutes.

Fighting them

  1. Stay quiet. Use melee or a suppressed weapon so one zombie doesn’t become ten.
  2. Block, then hit. Take the swing on your block and strike in the 1.5-second gap.
  3. Pick your hour. Every punch lands 1.5× harder at night. Ordinary zombies don’t run any faster in the dark.
  4. Have an exit. A vehicle or a room behind a locked code-locked door ends the fight.
  5. Patch up early. Getting knocked out near a zombie is deadly: it can feed on you, and the bites aren’t capped.

If a zombie kills you

  1. Five minutes pass. Your body lies where it fell.
  2. A zombie stands up where your body came to rest. If a zombie is still eating the body, it waits until the meal is over.
  3. It’s wearing your clothes and carrying the rest of your gear, and your body disappears.
  4. Kill it to get your kit back.
  • It happens to anyone a zombie kills: players, bandits and soldiers.
  • Anything taken from your body in those five minutes stays with whoever took it.
  • If something won’t fit on the zombie, it stays on your body, and the body stays too.
  • No maggots. A body that turns is gone before flies and maggots arrive, so a zombie kill leaves no fishing bait behind.
  • Killed by something else? Nothing gets up.
