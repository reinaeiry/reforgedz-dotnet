Wounds and Bleeding

Stop the bleeding, then heal. How IFAKs, splints, painkillers and eating well actually patch you up.

On this page

Health and Blood are two different rings. Bleeding drains Blood, and wounds cost Health. Stop the bleeding first, then heal with an IFAK, because your body barely heals on its own.

Health and Blood

  • Two rings, two problems. You can be at full Health and bleeding out, or at full Blood and badly hurt.
  • A pulsing bleeding icon at the bottom left means you’re losing blood.
  • A fracture icon appears there when a limb is badly hurt.
  • Blood shows through clothes. Anyone looking at you can see you’re bleeding.

Stop the bleeding

Item What it does How to get it
Bandage or field dressing Stops bleeding Loot, or craft from Cloth: 1 bandage in 15 s in the Crafts tab, or 3 in 30 s at a Workbench.
Tourniquet Stops bleeding from a limb Loot
IFAK Stops all bleeding at once, then heals you Loot. See below.

IFAKs

Hold the kit and use it. It checks you over and gives the right treatment:

  1. Bleeding? It bandages you.
  2. Hurt? It gives you morphine.
  3. Sick (25% or more) but not hurt? It hands you a painkiller dose.
  4. None of those? Nothing needs treatment right now, and no use is spent.

Every successful use also stops all bleeding and starts a 3-minute heal: 0.2% of your max health every second, 36% in total. The heal doesn’t push you past full, but anything that hurts you inside those 3 minutes gets healed too.

  • A hit ruins it. A gunshot, melee hit, crash or explosion cancels the heal and the morphine, and you’ll see Your treatment was interrupted! Get out of the fight first.
  • Cancel the animation and nothing is used.
  • Using a second kit while a heal is running extends it. The heal rate doesn’t double.
Kit Uses Where it turns up
IFAK, IFAK (Olive) 8 Medical and military buildings, Tier 2 areas and above
Small IFAK 4 Any building, Tiers 1 to 3

Painkillers

The Painkillers bottle holds 4 doses. Each lasts 3 minutes and:

  • Stops hurt legs slowing you. You can sprint on a broken leg.
  • Restores 25% of your shock, the damage that knocks you out.

A second dose while one is running resets the timer to 3 minutes. It doesn’t add to it. Painkillers heal nothing and don’t cure sickness. They turn up in any building type in Tiers 1 to 3.

Broken legs and splints

A badly hurt leg slows you to a limp, and walking on it makes you cry out every 2 to 4 minutes. Standing still is silent.

The Leg Splint fixes it:

  1. Craft one from 2 Sticks and 1 Cloth in your inventory (30 s). See Crafting.
  2. Apply it. Right-click it and choose Apply Splint, or drag it onto your leg slot. It goes on the worse leg by itself.
  3. Keep moving. While it’s on you can at least jog, and you stop crying out.
  4. Wait up to 10 minutes. The leg heals fully, and the splint is used up and disappears.
  • Any leg injury counts, not just a clean break.
  • Both legs hurt? Splint each one.
  • My legs are fine means neither leg needs one. Splint already applied means both legs already have one.
  • Bandits at high enough reputation heal faster in a splint.
  • In a hurry? Painkillers let you sprint instead of jog.

Healing on your own

Your Health only comes back by itself when all three are true:

  • Food and Water are both at 80% or higher.
  • Nothing has hurt you in the last 5 minutes.
  • You’re conscious.

Every 2 minutes you get back 0.1% of your max health, rising to 0.2% as both bars near full. That’s 3 to 6% an hour, so healing half your health this way takes 8 hours or more. Eat well to stay topped up, and carry an IFAK to actually heal.

Unconscious

  • Shock knocks you out. Bare fists drain it: about 10 unblocked punches put someone down.
  • Food and IFAKs can’t heal you while you’re out. A kit heal that was already running stops.
  • Zombies don’t stop. A zombie stops punching once you’re down and can start feeding on you instead, and its bites aren’t capped the way ordinary zombie punches are. See Zombies.
  • Block to stay up. Hold Middle Mouse, or the left trigger on a controller. Blocking cuts a bare-fist punch by 90% and a melee weapon hit by 30%. You need some stamina to start blocking, and holding it drains stamina.
