Keys and Locked Containers

Four coloured keys, four locked containers, and bunker keycards. What each opens, where keys come from, and what's inside.

On this page
  • How to open one
  • Where the containers are
  • Where keys come from
  • Keycards

Each coloured key opens one kind of locked container. Hold the key in your hands, open the container, and it always pays out: no search roll, guaranteed items, and a chance at caps. The key is used up.

Key Opens Guaranteed items Chance of caps Caps
Blue Key A locked military crate 10–12 weapons and explosives 75% 3,000–10,000
Red Key A locked locker 8 weapons, explosives, ammo and collectables 40% 2,000–6,000
Green Key Locked Cabinet 4–6 weapons 40% 1,000–4,000
White Key Safe 2–4 weapons, ammo and collectables 40% 500–3,000

How to open one

  1. Put the key in your hands. A key in your backpack or on your gear does nothing.
  2. Look at the container. Without the key the prompt says Locked and Requires Key in Hand! With the right key it says Unlock.
  3. Unlock it. The key disappears and the loot appears inside.
  • Take everything. Caps are only rolled when the loot first appears, not when someone reopens it.
  • It locks again. 30 minutes after it was opened, anything left inside is removed and the container locks until the next key.

Where the containers are

  • Red Key lockers stand in bandit camps on Chernarus and in a bandit stronghold on Faircroft Islands.
  • The Blue Key crate is inside the aircraft carrier’s tower. Each map has one.

Where keys come from

  • Loot. White and Green keys turn up in Tier 1 and Tier 2 areas, in most building types. Red and Blue keys turn up in Tier 2 and Tier 3 areas, in military, police and industrial buildings. A key left on the ground vanishes after 2 hours.
  • Bandits. Every bandit gets 1 or 2 rolls for an extra item, each with a 10% chance, and any of the four keys can be one of them.
  • The patrol helicopter. A helicopter flies a route between towns. Shoot it down and the crash site holds loot and guards, with a 50% chance that the pilot’s Blue or Red Key lies by the wreck. It isn’t marked on the map.
  • Not traders. No trader buys or sells keys.

Keycards

Keycards open bunker doors. They come in four levels, Level 01 to Level 04, and each bunker scanner needs its own level.

  1. Hold the keycard and use the scanner: Scan Keycard. With no keycard in your hands it shows Keycard Access Required and the card it needs, such as Level 01 Keycard. A wrong card flashes the scanner red and isn’t used up.
  2. The card is used up and a 5-minute countdown starts: Door Opening in 4m 59s. Every player on the server gets a security alert, and every siren on the map sounds for 5 minutes, including the raid sirens players build at their bases.
  3. The doors stay open for 30 minutes, with a warning 5 minutes before they shut.
  4. Once a bunker has opened, its scanner reads Bunker Already Looted until the server restarts.
  • Bunker crates stay shut until the doors open (Bunker Doors Closed). Each crate is filled once per restart: the first player to search it gets the loot, and anyone after can take what’s left.
  • Keycards are rare. They only spawn at a few rare-loot spots, such as Rify and the oil rig, and last 4 hours on the ground.
