Animals

Cows graze in herds on both maps and can be skinned for steaks. What you get from one, and what to do with the meat.

Cows are the wildlife you can use. They graze in herds out in the fields on both maps, they don’t fight back, and one is worth a pack full of steaks.

Where Pastures out in the farmland, on both maps
Herd size Four to a herd, in three sizes
What you get Up to 8 raw steaks from one cow
Then Cook them. Raw steak makes you ill

Skinning one

Kill the cow, walk up to it and use Skin Animal. The steaks go into your inventory, and they come out raw.

  • Cook before you eat. A raw steak is a good way to poison yourself, and a bout of food poisoning lasts 15 to 25 minutes. See Cooking and Spoilage and Fires and Cooking.
  • Eight steaks is a lot of food. One cow feeds you for a long stretch, so kill one near a fire rather than carrying the meat across the map while it spoils.
  • Herds come and go. A pasture holds one to three herds at a time, so a field that was empty an hour ago may not be.
