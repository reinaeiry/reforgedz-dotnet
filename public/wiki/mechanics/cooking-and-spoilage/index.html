Cooking and Spoilage

Cook stages, food poisoning, how long food lasts, and how fridges stop it rotting.

On this page
  • The five cook stages
  • How to cook
  • Food poisoning
  • Spoilage
  • Fridges

Only fully Cooked food is safe. Every other stage of meat and fish can poison you, and all food that can rot does so on the real clock, including while you’re offline.

The five cook stages

Cookable food moves through five stages, and its name tells you where it is: Raw Fish Fillet, Half Cooked Fish Fillet, Cooked Fish Fillet. Each stage also changes how much the food feeds you.

Stage Feeds you Food poisoning
Raw 40% of its full value Likely
Partly Cooked 60% Possible
Half Cooked 80% Possible
Cooked 100% Never
Burned 30% Small chance
Rotten (any stage) 20% Always for meat and fish, less on the Hero path

The exact odds for fish are on the Fishing page. Food keeps cooking after it reaches Cooked, and it burns if you leave it in.

How to cook

On a fire

  1. Build a Fireplace: 5 Sticks and 6 Stone in the crafting menu, 30 seconds. See Fires and Cooking.
  2. Light it. Food only cooks while the fire burns.
  3. Put the food in the fire’s storage. It holds up to 4 kg.
  4. Use Cooking on the lit fire to watch each item’s stage and progress, and take the food out once it’s Cooked.

On a fire, food spends 45 seconds in each stage unless it cooks at its own pace. Fish fillets do: their times are on Fishing.

On a cooker

The Cooker and Tiled Cooker are built from plans that traders sell. They need a running generator in range: see Power and Generators.

  • Use Turn Cooker On. With no generator running you’ll see No generator power.
  • Food spends 30 seconds in each stage, faster than a fire, except food with its own pace: fish fillets cook no faster here.
  • A cooker only takes food that can be cooked (Only food that can be cooked goes in here) and holds up to 8 kg.
  • If the generator stops, the cooker switches itself off. Turn it back on once the power returns.

Food poisoning

Each time you eat meat or fish that isn’t Cooked, the game rolls the odds for its stage. If you’re unlucky, nothing happens straight away. Symptoms start a few minutes later: 2 to 7 minutes after eating fish.

Once it starts:

  • You vomit every 1 to 2 minutes. Your character crouches, and each time you lose 8% food and 12% water.
  • It can’t starve you. Vomiting never takes either bar below 5%.
  • It doesn’t stack. Eating more bad food while you’re already poisoned doesn’t make it worse.
  • It wears off on its own. A bout lasts 15 to 25 minutes, then the vomiting stops.

Your standing changes the odds. On the Hero path your risk drops by up to 40%, and as a Bandit it rises by up to 40%. Your inventory’s perk panel shows your current figure as Food Poisoning Resistance.

Eating with bloody hands is a separate risk that shows up much later. See Sickness and Medicine.

Spoilage

Food rots on real time. The clock keeps running while you’re offline and through server restarts.

  • Fruit and vegetables (apples, pears, potatoes, carrots, tomatoes, peppers, pumpkin slices) rot in about 24 hours.
  • Whole fish rot in about 8 hours. Fillet times are on Fishing.
  • Canned food (sealed or opened), drinks and the Chocolate Bunny never rot.

Cooking buys time:

  • Cooked food rots three times slower than raw. Partly and Half Cooked food falls in between.
  • Reaching Cooked restarts the clock, so a fillet cooked just before it turned gets its full cooked life.
  • Burned food never rots.
  • Cooking can’t save rotten food. Once it’s rotten, it stays rotten.

Rotten food changes its look and gains (Rotten) in its name. It feeds you 20% of its value. Rotten meat and fish always poison you unless you’re on the Hero path, while rotten fruit and vegetables just barely feed you.

Fridges

A fridge stops food rotting completely while it has power. Build one from Fridge or Small Fridge plans sold by traders, and run a generator in range. See Power and Generators.

  • Power cut: a Fridge stays cold for 30 minutes and a Small Fridge for 20 minutes. After that, food inside rots at its normal rate until the power comes back.
  • Food and drink only. Anything else is refused with Only food and drink goes in the fridge.
  • Capacity: a Fridge holds up to 80 kg, a Small Fridge up to 25 kg.
  • Check it: the fridge tells you its state. Cooling - food stored here will not spoil. means you’re safe. Power lost - still cold for now. means restart the generator soon.
