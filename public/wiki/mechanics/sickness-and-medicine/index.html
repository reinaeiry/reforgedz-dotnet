Sickness and Medicine

What makes you sick, how the coughing kills you, and how to cure it with antibiotics without overdosing.

On this page

  • Immunity
  • Symptoms
  • Antibiotics
  • Getting better without pills
  • Food poisoning is different
  • Toxic gas is different
  • What doesn’t cure sickness

Sickness is a meter from 0 to 100% that builds while you’re too cold, too hot or dirty. Past 50%, every cough costs you health. Antibiotics clear it fast, and your own body clears it more slowly once you’re warm and fed.

Where you see it A sickness icon at the bottom left from 5%. Your inventory shows the exact number, or HEALTHY.
When it hurts From 50%: each cough costs health and you lose stamina
Cure Amoxicillin or Cephalexin, or rest somewhere warm
Overdose Too many doses too close together. You vomit away 50 points each of Food and Water.

What makes you sick

  • Being too cold or too hot. Sickness starts once your body temperature has stayed below 32 °C or above 40 °C for 25 minutes. Cold and wet together runs that clock twice as fast. Once it starts, an otherwise healthy player needs about another 100 minutes of exposure to reach 50%, and a player in poor shape about 40 minutes. Get back to a safe temperature and the 25-minute clock winds back twice as fast as it filled. See Body Temperature.
  • Eating with bloody hands. Butchering and melee fights leave blood on your hands. Eat like that and there’s a 35% chance you gain 8% sickness, 15 to 45 minutes later, long after you’ve forgotten the meal. Wash at a well or walk into water before you eat.
  • Zombie hits. A solid hit from a zombie has a 1 in 10 chance to add 8% sickness.

What stops it building

  • A roof or a closed vehicle. Sickness can’t build while you’re under a roof or sitting in a closed vehicle, even if you’re freezing.
  • The safe zone. Inside a safe zone sickness never builds, coughs cost no health and you lose no stamina. Antibiotics still work there.

Immunity

Your immunity is the average of five things: Food, Water, Health, Blood, and how close your body temperature is to 37 °C. It changes how fast everything happens.

Poor shape Average Fed, warm and unhurt
How fast sickness builds 1.6× 1× 0.4×
How strong a dose is 0.8× 1× 1.2×
How fast you recover without pills 0.25× 1.1× 2×

Below 40% immunity the 25-minute exposure clock also runs 1.5× faster.

Symptoms

Sickness What happens
5% The icon appears. Faint coughs, minutes apart.
50% Panic breathing starts. Every cough now costs health, and you keep losing stamina.
100% Coughing every 6 seconds or so. Left alone, it kills you.

While an antibiotic dose is working you keep coughing, but the coughs cost no health or stamina.

Antibiotics

Bottle Doses One dose works for One dose clears
Amoxicillin Bottle 6 7 min 30 s 43–65% sickness
Cephalexin Bottle 3 16 min 40 s 96–144% sickness: a full meter

The range depends on your immunity.

  • Doses stack. A second dose adds its time on top of whatever is left of the first.
  • A capsule icon shows while a dose is working and pulses in its last 30 seconds.
  • They work anywhere, even out in the cold, where your body can’t recover on its own.
  • Find them in medical and military buildings in Tier 2 and Tier 3 areas. See How Looting Works.

Overdose

Every dose adds to a drug load that your body clears at a steady rate. One Amoxicillin dose takes about 3 minutes to clear, and one Cephalexin dose counts as 2.2 Amoxicillin doses. Take a dose that pushes the load to four Amoxicillin doses’ worth and you overdose.

  • Two Cephalexin within about a minute of each other is an overdose.
  • Five Amoxicillin inside 3 minutes is an overdose.
  • Space your doses 3 minutes apart for Amoxicillin, or 7 minutes for Cephalexin, and the load never builds.

What an overdose does. The screen flashes. About 5 seconds later you drop to a crouch and vomit. Food and Water each drop by 50 points, and every dose you had working is gone. The drug load goes back to zero, so you can start again straight away, on an emptier stomach.

Getting better without pills

Once your temperature is back in the safe range, or you’re under a roof or in the safe zone, sickness drains away on its own while no dose is working.

Your shape Time to clear 50% sickness
Fed, warm and unhurt About 8 minutes
Average About 15 minutes
Poor Over an hour

Warning

Above 50%, the coughs keep hurting you while you wait it out. Only a working antibiotic or the safe zone stops that. If you’re past halfway, take a pill.

Food poisoning is different

Raw or undercooked food can give you food poisoning (see Cooking and Spoilage). It doesn’t use the sickness meter, and antibiotics do nothing for it.

  • It starts a few minutes after the meal, not straight away.
  • You vomit every 1 to 2 minutes. Each time costs 8 points of Food and 12 of Water.
  • It never drains either bar below 5%, so it won’t starve you to death by itself.
  • It doesn’t wear off on its own.
  • Eating more bad food while you’re poisoned doesn’t make it worse.

Toxic gas is different

Standing inside the toxic zone poisons you every second. Only a full hazmat set, both the head and the body, keeps it out.

  • The poison hurts your health directly. It doesn’t touch the sickness meter, and antibiotics do nothing for it.
  • Step out of the zone and the poison stops within a second.
  • A Toxic Zone icon on your map marks it: at Rify on Chernarus, and by the Brickworks on Faircroft Islands.

What doesn’t cure sickness

  • Painkillers only dull pain. See Wounds and Bleeding.
  • An IFAK used while you’re sick but unhurt hands you a painkiller dose. It doesn’t touch the meter.
