Terms

These are the terms between you and ReforgedZ, LLC for using reforgedz.net, playing on the ReforgedZ servers and buying from the shop. They are short on purpose. The monetization policy says what we sell and what we never will; the privacy policy says what we hold about you.

01 The server rules

Playing on our servers means playing by the server rules. They are posted in the rules channel of our Discord, where every player can read them before joining. Staff apply them in the game, in Discord and in the shop, and their decisions can be appealed through a ticket in Discord.

By buying you accept the server rules. A ban for breaking them ends paid perks without a refund.

02 Buying from the shop

  • Prices include VAT where it applies, at the rate of the country you buy from. The price you see at checkout is the price you pay.
  • PayPal is the payment processor. Every payment goes through PayPal, which handles your card or bank details. We never see them. You are charged in the currency shown at checkout; the other currencies the shop shows are estimates.
  • Subscriptions renew at the end of each period until you cancel. Cancel on your account page; what you paid for stays active until the period you paid for ends. If a renewal payment fails, the perk ends when that period ends.
  • Some products are limited per server. If the last slot is taken while you are paying, the purchase is not completed and any payment is refunded in full.
  • Delivery is in the game, against the in-game ID on your account, and on Discord where a product comes with a role. Keep both correct on your account page; we cannot deliver to an ID you did not give us.
  • Refunds are decided case by case, as the monetization policy says. A purchase ended by a ban is not refunded.

03 What a purchase is, and is not

Everything in the shop is cosmetic, or a convenience such as a priority place in the queue. Nothing sold gives an advantage over another player, and nothing in the game is locked behind a payment. A purchase buys the perk for the period shown, on the servers we run; it does not buy a say in how the servers are run, protection from the rules, or anything that survives a ban.

Bohemia Interactive permits server monetization under its own rules, and lists ReforgedZ as an approved server monetizer. That permission runs to January 31, 2027. If Bohemia's rules or its permission change, what we sell may change with them; anything already paid for is honoured for its period or refunded.

04 Your account

Your account is yours to look after: keep your sign-in to yourself, and tell us if you think someone else has used it. We may close an account that breaks the rules, is used to cheat or defraud, or is used to abuse staff or other players. Perks on a closed account end with it.

05 The servers

We run the servers for the community and keep them up as well as we can, but we do not promise uptime. Servers restart every four hours. Maps, mods and systems change as development goes on, and a change or a wipe can cost you progress, gear or a base. We tell you in Discord before a planned wipe.

Our mods are published on the Arma Reforger Workshop under their own licences, which say what you may do with them. Arma Reforger itself belongs to Bohemia Interactive, and you play it under Bohemia's own terms. This website is not affiliated or authorized by Bohemia Interactive a.s.

The site, the shop and the servers are provided as they are. As far as the law allows, we are not liable for loss that comes from an outage, a wipe, a change to the game or our mods, or a ban under the rules. Nothing in these terms takes away rights that consumer law gives you where you live.

These terms are governed by the law of Ohio, United States. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you keep the protection of your own country's consumer law.

07 Changes and contact

When these terms change, the new version is posted here with its date, and a change that affects purchases is announced in Discord first. Questions about a purchase go to contact@reforgedz.net or a ticket in Discord, which is faster.
