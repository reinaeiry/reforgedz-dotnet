Policy

Monetization

ReforgedZ is a community-driven project. Any optional purchases exist to support ongoing development and server costs. Nothing you can buy will give you an advantage over another player.

No Pay-to-Win

Every purchasable item is cosmetic. No stat changes, no gameplay advantages, no exceptions. A free player and a paying player are on the same level.

Fully Optional

Nothing is locked behind a paywall. All core gameplay, features, and content are available to every player regardless of whether they've spent anything.

BI Compliant

Everything here follows Bohemia Interactive's official monetization guidelines for Arma Reforger servers. Their rules take priority over ours if there's ever a conflict.

Nothing Here Gives You An Advantage

If it has the slightest chance of giving a buyer an edge, we don't sell it. Hard line, no exceptions. Items we will never put behind a paywall:

× Guns, mags, scopes, suppressors, or rounds
× Faster, tougher, or upgraded vehicles
× Helmets, plates, or anything that absorbs damage
× Ghillies, low-visibility camo, or stealth advantages
× Loot drops, supply caches, or inventory expansions
× XP boosts, level skips, or progression shortcuts
× Loot boxes, gacha pulls, or randomised rewards
× Paywalled maps, regions, or restricted servers
× Anything that changes how the game actually plays

Everyone on the server plays the same game. Cosmetics we sell render at default visibility and never grant a tactical perk, no quieter footsteps, no smaller hitbox, no extra concealment. If you have never spent a penny, you have access to every system, every map, and every event that a paying player does.

01 What This Covers

This policy applies to all optional paid content on ReforgedZ servers. The types of items we may offer include, but are not limited to, weapon skins, clothing cosmetics, custom assets like base flags or banners, and priority server slots. What's available may change over time as we add or retire items.

02 Cosmetic Items

Weapon skins change how a weapon looks. That's it. No damage changes, no recoil changes, no stat modifications of any kind. The same goes for clothing and character cosmetics. They're visual only and don't affect hitboxes, visibility to AI, or any other mechanic.

We keep cosmetics grounded in the setting. Some may be limited-run or tied to events. Players can submit their own designs for review, but everything goes through admin approval before it goes live.

03 Custom Assets

Players may be able to purchase personalised in-game items such as base flags, banners, or similar cosmetic props. These let you put your mark on the world without changing how anyone plays.

All custom content is reviewed and approved by the admin team. We reserve the right to reject anything we consider inappropriate, offensive, or disruptive to other players.

04 Payments & Refunds

Payments are processed through secure third-party platforms such as PayPal or card processors. We do not store your payment details ourselves.

Refund requests are handled on a case-by-case basis. If something went wrong with a purchase, get in touch and we'll sort it out. We're not here to take your money and ghost you.

05 Community & Feedback

If you think something we're selling crosses a line, or you've got ideas for what we should offer, we want to hear it. Monetization exists to keep the server running, not to alienate the community that plays on it.

We also run events and giveaways from time to time where cosmetics or slots are handed out for free.

06 Enforcement

Attempting to exploit, duplicate, resell, or abuse any purchased item or feature will result in an immediate and permanent ban. This includes using third-party tools or services. There are no warnings for this.

07 Contact

Questions, refund requests, or anything else related to purchases can be directed to contact@reforgedz.net or through our Discord. Discord will always be faster.

Data Protection & Legal

When you make a purchase through ReforgedZ, we may collect and store limited personal information necessary to process your transaction and deliver the purchased content. This may include your username, email address, transaction identifiers, and purchase history.

We do not sell, share, or distribute your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes. Payment processing is handled entirely by third-party providers (such as PayPal or Stripe) and we do not have access to or store your full payment card details.

Your data is stored securely and retained only for as long as necessary to provide the services you've purchased and to comply with any applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us.

By making a purchase, you acknowledge and agree to the terms outlined on this page. We reserve the right to update this policy at any time. Continued use of paid services after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

This policy is governed by and construed in accordance with applicable data protection regulations. For any data-related requests or concerns, contact us at contact@reforgedz.net.
