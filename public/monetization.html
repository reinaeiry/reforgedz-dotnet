Policy

Monetization

Our monetization framework for Arma Reforger ReforgedZ servers. Fair play, transparency, and full compliance with Bohemia Interactive guidelines.

1

Introduction

This monetization policy outlines the framework for selling priority slots, weapon skins, clothing skins, and other cosmetic items on our Arma Reforger ReforgedZ servers in compliance with community guidelines, legal standards, and fair play principles. The goal is to provide players with optional enhancements without creating unfair advantages or disrupting gameplay balance.

2

General Principles

  • Fair Play: Monetization features will not offer players competitive advantages over others. All sales will focus on cosmetic or convenience enhancements.
  • Transparency: All transactions and their associated benefits will be detailed on our website or marketplace.
  • Compliance: This policy adheres to Bohemia Interactive's monetization guidelines and terms of service for Arma Reforger servers.
  • Opt-In Enhancements: Participation in monetization features is entirely optional and does not restrict access to core gameplay.
3

Weapon Skins

  • Description: Players can purchase cosmetic skins for weapons. These skins alter only the appearance of the weapons and do not impact their functionality, damage, or stats.
  • Availability: Skins will be rotated regularly, including limited-time or event-exclusive skins.
  • Custom Skins: Players may submit custom designs for review and potential inclusion, subject to server admin approval.
4

Clothing Skins

  • Description: Players can customize their characters by purchasing cosmetic clothing skins. Like weapon skins, these skins do not alter gameplay mechanics or stats.
  • Variety: Clothing skins include a mix of survival-themed and stylistic options to enhance role-playing experiences.
5

Billboard Placements

  • Description: Players can purchase customizable billboard placements in the game world. These billboards do not alter gameplay mechanics or stats and are purely cosmetic.
  • Variety: Billboard placements allow players to display custom designs, messages, or artwork in designated in-game locations, subject to server admin approval for content and appropriateness.
6

Payment & Refund Policy

  • Payment Methods: Payments can be made through secure platforms such as PayPal, credit/debit cards, or other approved methods.
  • Refunds: Refunds will be issued only under specific circumstances. All refund requests must be submitted in writing.
7

Community Engagement

  • Feedback: The server team welcomes feedback on monetization practices and will adjust offerings based on community input.
  • Events & Giveaways: Free skins or priority slots may be awarded during server events, fostering a vibrant and inclusive player community.
8

Compliance with Bohemia Interactive

This monetization policy strictly adheres to Bohemia Interactive's official Arma Reforger Server Monetization Guidelines. Should any discrepancies arise, Bohemia Interactive's guidelines will take precedence.

9

Enforcement

Violation of this policy, including the use of third-party tools or unauthorized reselling of monetized features, will result in immediate account suspension or ban.

10

Contact Information

For questions or support regarding monetized features, please contact our support team at contact@reforgedz.net or visit our Discord to reach us.
