Policy

Monetization

ReforgedZ offers optional cosmetic purchases to help support ongoing development. Nothing you can buy will give you an edge in gameplay. This page explains what we sell, how it works, and the rules we follow.

01 What We Sell

Everything available for purchase on ReforgedZ is cosmetic. No stat boosts, no gameplay advantages, no pay-to-win. If you never spend a cent, your experience is the same as everyone else's.

Current and planned offerings include:

  • Priority server slots
  • Weapon skins that change appearance only, with no effect on damage, accuracy, or handling
  • Clothing and character cosmetics for personalising your survivor
  • Custom assets such as base flags, banners, and other personalisation items

02 Fair Play

Paid items do not change how the game plays. A player who has purchased skins and a player who hasn't are on completely equal footing when it comes to combat, survival, looting, and every other gameplay mechanic.

If we ever introduce something new to the store, it will follow this same rule. Cosmetic only, no exceptions.

03 Weapon Skins

Weapon skins are purely visual. They change how a weapon looks in your hands and on the ground. They do not change stats, bullet velocity, recoil, damage, or anything else that affects gameplay.

Skins rotate over time. Some may be limited-run or tied to events. Players can also submit their own skin designs for consideration. All submissions go through admin review before anything goes live.

04 Clothing & Character Cosmetics

Clothing skins let you change how your character looks. Different jackets, gear, headwear, survival-themed outfits, that kind of thing. None of it changes your hitbox, your stats, or your visibility to AI.

We aim to keep cosmetics grounded in the setting. You won't see neon spacesuits on Chernarus.

05 Custom Assets

Players can purchase custom in-game assets to personalise their experience. This includes things like custom base flags, banners, and similar items that let you leave your mark on the world without affecting gameplay.

All custom assets are subject to admin approval. We reserve the right to reject anything inappropriate, offensive, or disruptive.

06 Payments & Refunds

We accept payments through standard secure platforms including PayPal and card payments. All transactions are handled through trusted third-party processors.

Refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis. If you have an issue with a purchase, reach out to us directly. We're reasonable people and we'll work it out.

07 Community Input

We take feedback seriously. If the community has concerns about something we're selling or how we're selling it, we want to hear about it. Monetization should support the server, not undermine it.

We also run events and giveaways where cosmetics or priority slots are given out for free. Supporting the server financially is appreciated, but it's never required to have a good time.

08 Bohemia Interactive Compliance

This policy is written in line with Bohemia Interactive's official server monetization guidelines for Arma Reforger. If there's ever a conflict between this document and Bohemia's rules, their rules win. We stay current with their policies and will update this page if anything changes on their end.

09 Enforcement

Attempting to resell, exploit, or abuse any monetized feature will result in an immediate ban. This includes using third-party tools to duplicate or transfer purchased items. We don't do warnings for this kind of thing.

10 Contact

Got a question about a purchase, want to request a refund, or just need to talk to someone? Hit us up at contact@reforgedz.net or find us on Discord. Discord is faster.
