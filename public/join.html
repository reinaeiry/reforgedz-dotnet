How to join ReforgedZ

ReforgedZ runs inside Arma Reforger on PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox, and the three play together. Joining is one search and one download. Here is the whole thing, in order, with nothing left out.

01 Before you start

The download

The first join downloads about 20 GB of mods. Measured on 20 October 2026, it was 19.6 GB for the Chernarus servers and 19.3 GB for the Faircroft servers. The two maps share most of it, so trying the second map later is a much smaller download. Plan for the time it takes on your connection, and keep the game open while it runs.

Space on console

PlayStation 5 gives Arma Reforger 25 GB for mods in total, so ReforgedZ fits with little room left. If other modded servers' mods are already installed, remove them from the game's Workshop screen first. Xbox gives 50 GB, which holds ReforgedZ comfortably.

Permissions on console

A PlayStation or Xbox account needs permission to use user-generated content before the game will download mods. On a child or family-managed account this is a parental setting; on an adult account it is normally already on. If modded servers show as unavailable, this setting is the usual reason.

Crossplay

Our servers are crossplay. If crossplay is turned off in your game settings, the browser hides crossplay servers and you will not find us. Turn it on first.

02 Joining, step by step

  1. Open Multiplayer, then the Community tab

    From the main menu choose Multiplayer. Our servers are community servers, so they are not under the Official tab. Pick the Community tab, or stay on All.

  2. Search for ReforgedZ

    Type ReforgedZ into the search box. Four servers appear: EU1 and NA1 on Chernarus, EU2 and NA2 on Faircroft Islands. Pick the region nearest you. The four names are listed exactly on the official page; only those four are ours.

  3. Press Favourite

    Mark the server with the star before you join. The Recent tab forgets a server after a week; Favourites keep it, so next time it is one click away.

  4. Press Join and wait for the download

    The download starts the moment you press Join, and a window shows its progress. There is no separate install step. When the bar completes, the game connects on its own. If the server is full you are placed in a queue and connect when a slot frees up.

  5. If the join fails after the download

    Now and then a download finishes but the join fails, or the game reports a problem with the mods. The known fix is to clear the mods and let the game download them again.

    On PC: close the game, open Documents, My Games, ArmaReforger and delete the addons folder inside it. Start the game and join again. The download restarts from scratch, which is why it is worth trying a plain retry first.

    On PlayStation 5 and Xbox: open the game's Workshop screen, remove the ReforgedZ mods, then join the server again so they download fresh.

    Still stuck? Open a ticket in our Discord and say which platform you are on and what the game told you.

  6. Your first hour

    You spawn with almost nothing, and the first hour decides whether you keep it. The wiki's Your First Hour and Which Server Should I Join? are short and worth the read. Servers restart every four hours; the restart page says what that means for you. The server rules are short, and joining means you play by them.

03 Make sure it is really us

Anyone can put ReforgedZ in a server name. The official page lists the four exact names that are ours, every Workshop item we publish, and the one Discord invite we use: discord.gg/reforgedz. We never send friend requests or direct messages asking you to join somewhere else.
