Privacy policy

This page says what ReforgedZ, LLC holds about you when you use reforgedz.net and the shop, why we hold it, how long we keep it, who else sees it, and how to see or delete it. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

01 Who we are

The site, the shop and the game servers are run by ReforgedZ, LLC, a company registered in Ohio, United States. We are the controller of the data described here. You can reach us at contact@reforgedz.net, or through our Discord.

02 What this covers

This policy covers reforgedz.net, including the shop, the account area, the wiki, the map and the radio player. Our game servers and our Discord keep records of their own; section 09 says what they are. Discord's own privacy policy covers what Discord does with your account.

03 What we hold about an account

You only have an account if you created one, by signing in with Steam, by claiming an Xbox or PlayStation account, or by registering with an email address. Reading the site needs no account. For an account we hold:

  • Which platform you signed in with and its identifier: your Steam ID with the public name and avatar Steam shows, or your Xbox or PlayStation gamertag, or your email address and a scrambled form of your password that we cannot read back.
  • Your in-game ID, the identity the game gives your player, and the player name seen with it. Paid perks are delivered against it.
  • Your Discord ID, only if you linked Discord on your account page, so the Discord roles that come with a purchase can be given and taken away.
  • Your orders: what you bought, when, how much, whether it is active, refunded or cancelled, the PayPal references for the payment, and the email address PayPal used for the receipt.
  • The buyer's country, from two sources: the country your connection came from when you started checkout, and the country PayPal reports for you. Tax law requires us to keep two pieces of evidence that agree; only the two-letter country code is kept.
  • Your choice about email news, if you have made one on your account page. We send news by email only to people who chose it.

Steam, Xbox and PlayStation sign-in does not give us your email address. We have one only if you registered with it, added it to your account, or paid with it through PayPal.

04 Why we hold it

  • To run your account and let you sign in.
  • To deliver what you bought, in the game and on Discord, and to keep delivering it while a subscription runs.
  • To take payment through PayPal, send receipts and billing notices, and keep the records that tax and payment rules require.
  • To apply the server rules, including bans, and to handle refunds, disputes and chargebacks.
  • To answer support requests.
  • To send news by email, only where you chose it.
  • To keep the site and shop secure and working.

Where European or UK law applies, our basis is the contract with you for your account and purchases, our legal obligations for payment and tax records, our legitimate interest in keeping the servers fair and secure, and your consent for email news.

05 Cookies and storage on your device

The site sets no analytics cookies and no advertising cookies. No analytics script runs on these pages, and nothing on the site tracks you across other sites.

When you sign in, the site sets one cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. Xbox and PlayStation accounts get a second cookie that ties the account to the device that claimed it, which is how those accounts are protected. The shop remembers your chosen display currency, and the radio player remembers your favourites and queue, in your browser's own storage; neither leaves your device.

Cloudflare, which sits in front of the site, may set a technical cookie of its own to tell people from automated traffic. It is not used for analytics or advertising.

06 Who else sees it

  • PayPal processes every payment. We never see your card or bank details. PayPal gives us your payment reference, the email address and name on your PayPal account, and your country.
  • Steam, when you sign in with it, confirms who you are and gives us your public name and avatar.
  • Discord, when you link it, gives us your Discord ID, and we tell Discord which roles to give your account.
  • Cloudflare carries every request to the site and protects it. It sees your IP address and the pages you request.
  • Google Fonts serves the two typefaces the site uses, so your browser requests them from Google's servers. The map page loads its map library from unpkg.com in the same way.

Your data is held on servers that ReforgedZ rents and controls, and it may be processed in the United States, where ReforgedZ, LLC is based.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We share it with the authorities only when the law requires it.

07 How long we keep it

  • Account details stay for as long as the account exists. Ask and we delete the account, apart from what the next two lines require us to keep.
  • Orders and payment records are kept for as long as tax and payment rules require, including the country evidence that goes with them.
  • Ban records are kept for as long as the ban stands, so it stays in place.
  • Security records of who claimed a console account, and from where, are kept for a year. Records of staff actions on an account are kept with the account.
  • Technical logs of requests to the site, which include your IP address, are kept for a short time to keep the site running and secure.

08 Your rights

Whoever you are and wherever you live, you can:

  • See what we hold about you, and get a copy of it.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete your account and your data, apart from the records section 07 says we must keep.
  • Object to any use of your data, and stop email news at any time.

Email contact@reforgedz.net from the address on your account, or from the PayPal address you paid with, and say what you want. We answer within a month. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom you can also complain to your data-protection authority.

09 On the game servers and Discord

Our game servers keep their own records of who played: the identity the game gives your player, the name you used, when you joined and left, and what happened when it matters for the rules. Staff use them to run the servers, answer reports and apply bans. When someone is banned, we keep the identifiers needed to keep the ban in place, which can include the addresses the game connected from.

Support tickets in our Discord are kept so staff can see the history of a case. What you post in Discord is held by Discord under its own policy.

10 Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the new version is posted here with its date. A change that affects what we hold about you, or why, is announced in our Discord first.
