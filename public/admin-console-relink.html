Console re-link

For console players who cannot sign in because their gamertag is already linked and they cleared cookies or changed device. Generates a single-use link that hands the account lock to whatever device opens it.

Check who you are talking to first. A gamertag is public, so anyone can claim one. This link gives whoever opens it full access to that account and its purchases.

Issuing a new link immediately retires any previous unused one for that player, so if a link ends up somewhere it should not, just generate another.
